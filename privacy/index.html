Privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026.

This site collects as little as it can. It sets no advertising cookies, does not follow you to other sites, and sells nothing about you to anyone.

Who runs this site

homeschoolaitutor.com, also called Homeschool AI Tutor or HomeschoolAITutor, is operated by MC AI Studio LLC (442 Lorimer St PMB 283, Brooklyn, NY 11206). The site describes 12-week AI tutor guides and printables. Paid guides are sold only through our Etsy shop, HomeschoolAITutor.

What you give us, and why

  • Newsletter sign-up. If you submit the email form on this site, we receive the address you enter so we can send the updates you asked for. The form is processed by Kit (ConvertKit). You can unsubscribe from any email. Kit's privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • Email you send us. If you write to education@michaelcarroll.studio or hello@michaelcarroll.studio, we keep the address and message long enough to reply and to handle your request.
  • Purchases. If you buy a guide, the purchase is made on Etsy, not on this site. Etsy handles payment and order information under Etsy's privacy policy. We do not receive your card details.

What is collected automatically

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless visit counter. It helps us see how many people use the pages. It is not used for advertising and does not follow you to other sites. Cloudflare's privacy policy covers that service.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. We share it only if the law requires it, or with a service that runs a part of the site (Kit for the newsletter, Cloudflare for analytics, GitHub for hosting, Etsy for shop orders), and then only to provide that service.

How long we keep it

Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Email you send us is kept only as long as needed to reply and to keep a record of the request.

Your rights

You can ask what we have about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Email education@michaelcarroll.studio or hello@michaelcarroll.studio. We will reply within a few days.

Children

The guides are written for parents, carers, and educators. This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe we have, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the new policy is posted here.
