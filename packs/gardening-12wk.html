Safety

This is educational content, not professional advice. Handle tools carefully and read and follow every fertilizer and pesticide label — wear gloves, eye protection, and a mask when a label calls for it. Never eat any plant, berry, leaf, or mushroom you haven't positively identified as safe. Watch sun and heat exposure, hydrate, and mind your allergens. For regional advice on climate, pests, and what's safe to grow and eat, consult your local cooperative extension service.
