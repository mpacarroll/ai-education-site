Nobody is "bad at math." They have gaps, and gaps compound. This 12-week plan uses an AI tutor's endless patience to quietly go backwards to the exact missing piece, then rebuild, with no embarrassment, no lectures from you. Every week is mastery-gated: you move on because the learner showed you, out loud, not because the calendar said so.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each with a plain-language concept explanation, a concrete worked example, a timed, minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor instructions built in (starting with a launch prompt that tells the AI to diagnose before it teaches), a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, optional independent practice, and a note for you.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes, no paid plan needed.
  • A parent/teacher orientation: your job isn't to explain math; it's one fully scripted ~60-minute session a week. Every step is timed and written out for you, and the single most useful sentence you'll say all season is "show me how you'd start." No math expertise needed on your part.
  • An AI tutor that teaches, never tells, plus four rescue prompts for the moments things go sideways: a "drop to friendlier numbers" reset when the learner is stuck or frustrated, a never-give-the-answer hint ladder when the AI starts handing out answers, a puzzle mode for boredom, and a fresh-problem check that they really understand.
  • A gap map the learner builds in week 1 (solid, shaky, missing) and crosses off on Demo Day in week 12.

Who it's for

Parents and teachers guiding a learner aged roughly 9–14 who says they're "bad at math," and math-anxious adults who never got their footing. No math confidence required on your part; the tutor teaches, you lead.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A chatbot will hand over the answer and skip the gap that caused the struggle. This pack does the opposite: it diagnoses the missing piece first, drops to friendlier numbers when frustration hits, and gates every week behind a checkpoint the learner shows out loud. That's how confidence actually gets rebuilt: structure a raw chatbot doesn't have.

FAQ

  • Do I need to be good at math? No. The tutor diagnoses and teaches; you set the pace and ask for the "show me."
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Any modern AI assistant works (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini), and the free version of any of them is enough. The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes, before week 1 starts.
  • Is my child's data collected? Not by us. It's a digital guide sold to you, the adult, with no login and no child account. You start and supervise each session under your own AI account, and the guide explains what not to share in a chat.
  • What ages? Roughly 9–14, and math-anxious adults.
  • Does it work outside the US? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every launch prompt tells the AI to use your country or region (given, inferred, or asked) for spelling, units, money, school terms, and examples.
  • Refunds? Standard marketplace refund policy applies.
