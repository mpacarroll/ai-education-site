Math That Makes Sense — A 12-Week Guide to Rebuilding Math Confidence With an AI Tutor.

Short description (above the fold)

Nobody is "bad at math" — they have gaps, and gaps compound. This 12-week plan uses an AI tutor's endless patience to quietly go backwards to the exact missing piece, then rebuild — no embarrassment, no lectures from you. Every week is mastery-gated: you move on because the learner showed you, out loud, not because the calendar said so.

What you get

  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes — no paid plan needed.
  • A parent/teacher orientation: your job isn't to explain math — it's one fully scripted ~60-minute session a week. Every step is timed, every AI prompt is written for you, and the single most useful sentence you'll say all season is "show me how you'd start." No math expertise needed on your part.
  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each with a plain-language concept explanation, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan, copy/paste AI-tutor prompts at every step (including the diagnostic-first starter prompt), a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, and independent practice.
  • An AI-tutor prompt playbook — a Socratic tutor that diagnoses before it teaches, a "drop to friendlier numbers" rescue for frustration, a puzzle-mode for boredom, and a never-give-the-answer guardrail.
  • Four front-matter rescue prompts for the moments things go sideways (learner shuts down, answer without explanation, wrong prerequisite gap, session running long), plus the gap map the learner builds in week 1 and revisits as they go.

Who it's for

Parents and teachers guiding a learner aged roughly 9–14 who says they're "bad at math" — and math-anxious adults who never got their footing. No math confidence required on your part; the tutor teaches, you lead.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A chatbot will hand over the answer and skip the gap that caused the struggle. This pack does the opposite: it diagnoses the missing piece first, drops to friendlier numbers when frustration hits, and gates every week behind a checkpoint the learner shows out loud. That's how confidence actually gets rebuilt — structure a raw chatbot doesn't have.
