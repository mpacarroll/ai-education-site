TOK and the Extended Essay are the two pieces of the IB Diploma no textbook really teaches — they ask for original thinking and an independent research argument. This is a done-for-you 12-week plan that builds both: the TOK habits of mind (areas of knowledge, the knower, perspective and justification, the exhibition, the prescribed-title essay) interwoven with taking an Extended Essay from a research question to a defended draft and viva.

Format

Self-paced, 12 weeks. You lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week — every step timed and every AI prompt written out for you, so no subject expertise is needed. Works with any modern AI chat assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini); free versions are enough, and the guide's opening section walks a complete beginner through setup in about five minutes.
