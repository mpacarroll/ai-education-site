Who it's for

Adults who write and want genuine command of the sentence, well past half-remembered grammar rules. If your sentences work but you can't say why, or don't work and you can't say why not, this gives you the x-ray vision to see the structure and move it on purpose. No linguistics background assumed; every term is introduced through your own writing.

How this is different

This is not a grammar course and not a workplace-writing course. Grammar tells you what's allowed; this teaches you how meaning is built: how clauses carry the core, how modifiers attach, how coordination and subordination show which idea leads, why ambiguity happens, how rhythm and length place emphasis, and how punctuation signals the wiring. You come out able to construct, diagnose, and control any sentence for a deliberate effect, a skill that sits under every kind of writing.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

Ask a raw chatbot to "fix my sentence" and it quietly rewrites it, and you learn nothing. This pack ships the structure and guardrails it lacks: a real 12-week progression from the core outward, a repeatable diagnosis method, and a tutor set up to have you find the skeleton, pin down the structural fault, and coach you to rebuild, never to hand you a finished line. When a sentence is ambiguous, it has you name both readings the structure allows and makes you choose. Command you keep, not a fix you close the tab on.

  • Is this the same as a business-writing course? No. That's about workplace documents: emails, reports, the ask on top. This is the craft of the sentence itself, the layer beneath any writing you do.
