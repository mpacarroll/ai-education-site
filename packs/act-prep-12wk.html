Who it's for

High-school students preparing for the ACT who want a plan, not just a pile of practice questions, whether this is your first attempt or you're retaking it and need to know exactly where the remaining points are.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A free chatbot will answer whatever question you paste in, which feels productive but skips the part that actually raises scores: knowing where your real gaps are, practicing under the clock, and reviewing every miss instead of moving on. This pack ships the structure a raw chatbot lacks: an adaptive diagnosis that sorts every section into solid/shaky/missing, a real 12-week progression from drill to full timed practice, and a tutor that reviews the why behind every miss instead of just marking it wrong. A prep book can't adapt to your gaps; this can, and it builds timed practice (on your own timer) into every week.

FAQ

  • Is this official ACT material? No. This pack is not affiliated with or endorsed by ACT, Inc. or any test maker, and it contains no official test content. Use it alongside official ACT practice tests and the current official format, which is the source of truth for content and rules.
  • Will this guarantee my score? No. No pack, book, or tutor can guarantee a score or an admission outcome. This plan gives your effort real structure (diagnose, drill, time, review) and honest score tracking, not a promise.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Any modern AI chat assistant works: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini. The free version of any of these is enough; no paid plan is needed. If you've never used one before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes before Week 1 starts.
  • Do I need to already be strong in every section? No. Week 1 is a full diagnostic that finds your real starting point, and the middle weeks drill exactly what's shaky or missing.
  • What if my score plateaus? The guide includes a rescue prompt for a stalled section: the tutor looks at your actual error pattern and changes the approach instead of just repeating the same drills.
  • Does it cover the optional essay? Only as part of test-day practice. Week 12's dress rehearsal includes the optional essay if you're taking it, but the plan doesn't teach essay writing.
