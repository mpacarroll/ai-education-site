A masterclass you actually finish, because you spend it selling, not watching someone else talk about selling. This 12-week plan turns an AI tutor into a real buyer: curious, skeptical, price-sensitive, sometimes already leaning toward a competitor. You practice honest discovery, clear value framing, and objection-handling against real pushback, then the tutor drops character and debriefs what landed. By the end you can walk a buyer from open to close without ever feeling like you're pushing. This isn't the conflict-resolution pack; nobody's mad at you here, they just haven't decided yet, and your job is to help them decide well.

Format: A deep digital guide: twelve weekly sessions, each about 60 minutes, fully scripted end to end. You lead the session; the guide tells you exactly what to do, with the AI-tutor prompts built into each step. The words you say to the buyer are always your own. No expertise needed, no live sessions, no group calls.

Who it's for

Adults who have to sell something (a product, an idea, or themselves) and hate feeling pushy. Founders pitching a product, salespeople who want a method instead of a script, freelancers selling their own work, and anyone who has to pitch an idea, a raise ask, or themselves in an interview. No sales background needed; feeling awkward about selling is the starting line, not a disqualifier.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will hand you a script and cheerfully tell you it's persuasive. This pack ships the structure it lacks: a real 12-week progression, a tutor set up to play a genuinely skeptical buyer (pushing back, comparing you to a competitor, raising real objections), so you rehearse against resistance instead of reading tips. It asks what the buyer actually needs before it coaches a single line, and it will call you out if you reach for pressure or false urgency instead of honest value. Reps against a real buyer, not confident guessing.

Format note

This is a paid pack framed like a masterclass, and built to feel like one you finish. The "classroom" is you and an AI tutor role-playing a real sale, one scripted ~60-minute session a week, with every step timed and written out for you. The value isn't watching a pitch deck; it's the reps you get discovering, framing, and closing out loud before it counts.

FAQ

  • Do I need any sales experience? None. Week 1 starts with your own mindset about selling; every skill is rehearsed in small, low-stakes reps before you use it for real.
  • I've never used an AI chat assistant. Which one does it use, and can I still do this? Yes. It works with any modern AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini), and the free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. The guide's first section walks a complete beginner through setup in about five minutes before Week 1 starts. Voice mode helps if you want to practice out loud, but isn't required.
  • I'm not in the US. Will it work for me? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for spelling, money, and examples.
  • Will this teach me manipulation tactics or "dark pattern" closing tricks? No, the opposite. The tutor is set up to flag pressure tactics, false urgency, or fudged details if you reach for them, and route you back to an honest discovery question instead. The whole method is: understand the need, frame the real value, and let an honest "no" stand when it's the right answer.
  • Will it just give me a script to memorize? No, that's the point. The tutor makes you find your own words by asking what the buyer needs first, then role-plays so you earn the pitch by saying it under a little real pushback.
