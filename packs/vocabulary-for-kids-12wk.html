A Bigger Vocabulary — A 12-Week Guide to Growing a Child's Vocabulary With an AI Tutor.

Short description (above the fold)

Flashcards and word lists fade by Friday. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for growing a 9–13-year-old's vocabulary the smart way — through context clues, word roots and families, and real use — so they can figure out new words on their own instead of memorizing a list for a test, using an AI tutor to ask, explain, and play word games while you do the leading. You do not need to be a vocabulary expert.

What you get

  • "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" — a five-minute onboarding in the front matter that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready, using a free AI assistant. No paid plan needed.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when a session goes sideways — frustration, boredom, an off-track answer, or a stuck moment — so you're never left improvising.
  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each built to run for about 60 minutes and fully scripted: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan, copy/paste AI-tutor prompts at every step, a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, and practice.
  • You lead one ~60-minute session each week. Every step is timed and every AI prompt is written for you — no vocabulary or teaching expertise needed.

The 12 weeks

  1. Words Are Everywhere — vocabulary is a daily habit, not a list
  2. Becoming a Word Detective — guessing meaning from context clues
  3. Word Roots & Families — one root unlocks a whole family of words
  4. Building Blocks: Prefixes — predicting meaning before checking
  5. Building Blocks: Suffixes — taking words apart, piece by piece
  6. Synonyms & Shades of Meaning — picking the exact right word
  7. Bigger Words & When to Use Them — upgrading tired words on purpose
  8. Words for Feelings & Ideas — a richer vocabulary for the inside stuff
  9. Keeping a Personal Word Collection — the habit that makes it stick
  10. Project Week I: Plan the Ten-Word Challenge (the learner's own word list)
  11. Project Week II: Use Them For Real — in real conversation and writing
  12. Demo Day: The Word Showcase — present, celebrate, choose what's next

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 9–13. No vocabulary or English-teacher background needed — you lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session a week, plus a spot to keep the word jar where everyone can see it.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily hand your kid a definition the moment they ask — which teaches nothing and doesn't build the skill of figuring a word out. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc from context clues through roots, prefixes, and suffixes to ten words genuinely put to use, Socratic prompts that make the tutor ask the learner to guess first, guardrails that stop it from just handing over the meaning, and checkpoints that prove the learner can do it themselves. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.
