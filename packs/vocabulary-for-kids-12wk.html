A Bigger Vocabulary

Ages 9-13 · Reading and writing

Take the free placement check first: 15 minutes, and it names the week to start at.

Flashcards and word lists often fade fast. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for growing a 9–13-year-old's vocabulary the smart way (through context clues, word roots and families, and real use), so they can figure out new words on their own instead of memorizing a list for a test, using an AI tutor to ask, explain, and play word games while you do the leading. You do not need to be a vocabulary expert.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each built to run for about 60 minutes and fully scripted: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, and optional practice.
  • "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here": a five-minute onboarding in the front matter that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready, using a free AI assistant. No paid plan needed.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when a session goes sideways (a frustrated learner, an AI that hands over definitions too soon, boredom, and checking real understanding), so you're never left improvising.
  • You lead one ~60-minute session each week. Every step is timed and written out for you, with no vocabulary or teaching expertise needed.

The 12 weeks

  1. Words Are Everywhere: vocabulary is a daily habit, not a list
  2. Becoming a Word Detective: guessing meaning from context clues
  3. Word Roots & Families: one root unlocks a whole family of words
  4. Building Blocks: Prefixes (predicting meaning before checking)
  5. Building Blocks: Suffixes (taking words apart, piece by piece)
  6. Synonyms & Shades of Meaning: picking the exact right word
  7. Bigger Words & When to Use Them: upgrading tired words on purpose
  8. Words for Feelings & Ideas: a richer vocabulary for the inside stuff
  9. Keeping a Personal Word Collection: the habit that makes it stick
  10. Project Week I: Plan the Ten-Word Challenge (the learner's own word list)
  11. Project Week II: Use Them For Real (in real conversation and writing)
  12. Demo Day: The Word Showcase (present, celebrate, choose what's next)

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 9–13. No vocabulary or English-teacher background needed. You lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session a week, plus a spot to keep the word jar where everyone can see it.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily hand your kid a definition the moment they ask, which rarely sticks and doesn't build the skill of figuring a word out. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc from context clues through roots, prefixes, and suffixes to ten words genuinely put to use, Socratic prompts that make the tutor ask the learner to guess first, guardrails that stop it from just handing over the meaning, and checkpoints that prove the learner can do it themselves. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need to know a lot of fancy words myself? No. The AI tutor explains and plays the word games; you lead and celebrate. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Any modern AI assistant works (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini), and the free version of each is enough. If you've never used an AI chat assistant before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes, no experience assumed.
  • Is my child's data collected? No. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise each session under your own account. The AI assistant you choose has its own privacy policy, so the guide shows you what to keep out of chats.
  • Does this replace spelling or memorizing word lists for school? It complements it. The goal here is understanding (context, roots, and real use), which makes memorized lists stick better and helps with words that were never on any list at all.
  • We're not in the US. Does it work? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every AI prompt tells the assistant to use your country or region for spelling, units, money, school terms, and examples.
  • What ages? Roughly 9–13.
