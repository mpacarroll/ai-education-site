This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding a 9–13-year-old through the real memory techniques that make schoolwork (and everything else) stick: turning boring facts into silly pictures that are impossible to forget, building a memory palace out of a place they already know by heart, and chunking big lists into bite-sized groups — with an AI tutor that drills and quizzes playfully, like a game, until the recall is real. You do not need a good memory yourself to lead this.

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 9–13 who forgets things they "should" remember, or who just wants to get sharper at holding on to facts, names, and lists. No memory-expert background needed, and no prior AI experience needed — you lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and every AI prompt written for you.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily just tell your child the answer — which trains nothing. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc through the actual techniques memory champions use (silly pictures, a memory palace, chunking), a tutor that quizzes for true cold recall instead of accepting "that looks right," guardrails that keep it from handing over answers, and checkpoints that prove the trick worked. Curriculum + real technique + a way to see progress. That's the difference.
