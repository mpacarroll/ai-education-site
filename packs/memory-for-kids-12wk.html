Take the free placement check first: 15 minutes, and it names the week to start at.

Every kid can have a great memory; it's a trainable skill, not a trait. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding a 9–13-year-old through the real memory techniques that make schoolwork (and everything else) stick: turning boring facts into silly pictures that are hard to forget, building a memory palace out of a place they already know by heart, and chunking big lists into bite-sized groups, with an AI tutor that drills and quizzes playfully, like a game, until the recall is real. You do not need a good memory yourself to lead this.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each built to run in about 60 minutes: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint for real cold recall (not just recognition), common mistakes to watch for, and practice.
  • "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here": a five-minute onboarding in the front matter that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready, using a free AI assistant (no paid plan needed). Plus four rescue prompts for when a session goes sideways.
  • A digital guide, not a stack of printables: everything you need (concepts, examples, scripts, prompts, checkpoints) is written into each session itself.

The 12 weeks

  1. Your Memory Can Grow: memory as a trainable skill, not a fixed trait
  2. Making Pictures That Stick: the silly-picture trick
  3. The Memory Palace: placing things in a spot you know by heart
  4. Remembering Names: a three-step trick for new names
  5. Numbers and Facts: turning digits into pictures and rhymes
  6. Chunking Big Lists: breaking long lists into memorable groups
  7. Remembering for School: spelling words, vocabulary, times tables
  8. Mixing the Tricks: combining tricks on bigger material
  9. Practicing So It Lasts: why re-testing beats re-reading
  10. Project Week I: Plan a Memory Feat
  11. Project Week II: Build the Feat
  12. Family Demo Day: perform it, then pick the next goal

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 9–13 who forgets things they "should" remember, or who just wants to get sharper at holding on to facts, names, and lists. No memory-expert background needed, and no prior AI experience needed. You lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and written out for you.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily just tell your child the answer, which trains nothing. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc through the actual techniques memory champions use (silly pictures, a memory palace, chunking), a tutor that quizzes for true cold recall instead of accepting "that looks right," guardrails that keep it from handing over answers, and checkpoints that prove the trick worked. Curriculum + real technique + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need a great memory myself? No. The AI tutor teaches and quizzes; you lead by showing up and asking for the checkpoint. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Works with any modern AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini), and a free version is enough; no paid plan needed. The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes, before Week 1 starts.
  • Is my child's data collected? Not by us. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise every AI session yourself, under your own account. The AI provider you choose may store chats under its own policy, so the guide has you share only a first name and age, never an address or phone number.
  • What ages? Roughly 9 to 13. Every launch prompt gives the tutor your learner's age, so it pitches the examples and pace to fit.
