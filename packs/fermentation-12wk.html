This is educational content, not food-safety certification. Fermentation carries real risks, including botulism, when ratios or cleanliness go wrong. The pack teaches tested salt and acid ranges, full submersion, and how to recognize spoilage — and the rule that governs all of it: when in doubt, throw it out.
