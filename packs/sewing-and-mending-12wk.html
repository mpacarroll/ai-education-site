Safety

This is educational content, not professional instruction. Needles, pins, scissors, and sewing machines can injure — keep fingers clear of the needle, unplug the machine when threading it or clearing a jam, and always cut away from yourself. Work at a pace where you can stop the instant something feels wrong.
