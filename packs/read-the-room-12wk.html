Who it's for

Adults who want to read situations and people accurately and come across the way they intend: at work, at home, in new rooms. No background in psychology needed; if you've ever misread a meeting, a date, or a family gathering and wanted to know why, this is built for you.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily hand you a "10 body language tricks" list lifted from folklore, and let you treat one gesture as proof of a hidden thought. This pack ships the structure and rigor it lacks: a real 12-week progression through the actual science, a tutor trained to make you name your baseline and your cluster before you draw any conclusion, and a guardrail that refuses to turn this into a manipulation toolkit even if you ask. Genuine reading skill, built on evidence, not confident guessing dressed up as insight.

Format note

This is a paid pack framed like a masterclass, and built to feel like one you finish. The "classroom" is you and an AI tutor working through real research and real interactions: one fully-scripted ~60-minute session a week, with every step timed and written out for you. The value isn't watching a lecture on microexpressions; it's learning to read real signals honestly, in context, on people you know.

FAQ

  • Do I need any experience? None, with reading people or with AI tools. Week 1 starts with your own snap-judgment habits and the one rule that fixes them; every skill builds from there in small, checked steps.
  • I've never used an AI chat assistant. Is that a problem? No. The guide opens with a "Start here" section that walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini all work, and free versions are enough; no paid plan needed. Each session's tutor instructions are already written into the guide, so there's no special software to learn. Voice mode is handy when your hands are busy but isn't required.
  • Is this NLP, mentalism, or "how to detect lies"? No. It's grounded in published research on nonverbal communication, and it explicitly debunks lie-detection folklore and NLP-style claims that don't hold up. You'll learn what the evidence actually supports and its real limits.
  • Will this teach me to manipulate people? No, the opposite. Every week is framed around honest understanding and connection, and the tutor is set up to refuse manipulation tactics even if you ask for them, redirecting you back to the real method.
  • Is this therapy? No. It's a skills course, not therapy or a diagnosis of anyone. If social situations cause you real distress, the guide points you to a qualified professional.
