Most kids meet civics as a list of vocabulary words to memorize for a test, not as a system that actually shapes their lives. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding a 12–16-year-old from "why do we even have government?" to running a real civics project on an issue they care about, using an AI tutor that explains the branches, the rights, the laws, and the vote, strictly non-partisan and fact-based the whole way. You do not need a civics or poli-sci background.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each built to run in one focused ~60-minute sitting: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, and independent practice.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes. It works with any free AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini); no paid plan needed.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for the moments every session hits: a frustrated learner, a bored one, an AI that takes a side or hands over the answer, and a quick check that the learner really understands. Each one gets the session back on track.
  • A digital guide, fully scripted: every step timed and written out for you. You lead one ~60-minute session a week; no civics or poli-sci expertise needed, because the guide and the tutor do the explaining.

The 12 weeks

  1. Why We Have Government: a friendly diagnostic and the idea of the social contract
  2. Rights and Responsibilities
  3. Meet the Three Branches: legislative, executive, judicial
  4. Checks and Balances: how no branch gets too powerful
  5. How a Bill Becomes a Law: the real journey, step by step
  6. Voting and Elections
  7. Local vs. National Government: who's actually responsible for what
  8. Being an Active Citizen: participation beyond the ballot box
  9. Disagreeing Well in a Democracy
  10. Civics Project I: Plan It (a real issue, a real plan)
  11. Civics Project II: Do It
  12. Demo Day and the Next Civic Goal

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged 12–16 who should understand how their own government actually works. No civics or poli-sci background needed on your part: you lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session a week, every step timed and written out for you; the tutor does the explaining and the diagnosing.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily hand over an opinion on a hot-button issue (or the whole answer to a worksheet), which teaches nothing and risks steering a kid's politics instead of their understanding. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that builds from why government exists up through a genuine civics project, a tutor that diagnoses before it teaches, guardrails that keep it strictly non-partisan and stop it from handing over answers, and checkpoints that prove the learner can explain it themselves. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need a civics or poli-sci background? No. The AI tutor explains and diagnoses; you lead and ask for the "show me." Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Which AI does it use? I've never used one. Any modern AI assistant works: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini. The free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. The guide's first section, "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here," walks a complete beginner through setup in about five minutes.
  • Is this pack partisan? Will it push my kid toward a party or a position? No. Every prompt in the guide instructs the tutor to stay strictly non-partisan and factual. It teaches how government works, not what to think about any issue, party, or candidate.
  • Is my child's data collected? No. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise every AI session under your own account.
  • We don't live in the US. Does it still work? Yes. The guide is written in US English and uses the US federal system as its worked example, but every prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for spelling, units, money, school terms, and examples. The weeks built on the US system also tell it to re-map each institution (parliament, head of state, courts, elections) to your own government.
  • What ages? Roughly 12–16. Works well for any learner ready to understand how their own government actually works, regardless of grade level.
