Turn "can I take the picture?" into a real eye for photography. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding a 10–14-year-old from pointing-and-shooting to actually seeing a photo (light, framing, the moment), so their pictures go from snapshots to real photographs, using any phone or camera they already have. An AI tutor coaches the craft while you lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and written out for you. You do not need to know anything about photography, and you do not need to have ever used an AI chat assistant before.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each built to run in about 60 minutes and fully scripted: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint so you always know it landed, common mistakes to watch for, and practice to lock it in.
  • "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here": a five-minute onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready, using a free AI assistant (no paid plan needed). Plus a parent/teacher orientation on why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when a session goes sideways: the learner is frustrated or bored, the AI starts composing the shot for them, or you want to check they really understand and didn't just get lucky.
  • An AI-tutor playbook: a Socratic launch prompt for each session, with a guardrail built in that keeps the tutor coaching instead of composing the shot for the child.
  • Works wherever you live: the guide is written in US English, and every launch prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for spelling, units, money, and examples.
  • Safety and privacy built in: photos stay with family, pets, and everyday surroundings, with no strangers and nothing posted publicly without your say-so, and you run every AI session under your own account.

The 12 weeks

  1. Snapshot or Photograph? What actually makes a photo good
  2. Meet Your Camera: holding steady, basic controls
  3. Light Is Everything
  4. Framing & Getting Close
  5. The Moment: anticipating instead of only posing
  6. Angles & Point of View
  7. Telling a Story in One Picture
  8. People & Pets: natural, not stiff
  9. Editing Simply: three edits, no filters
  10. Photo Project I: Plan It (a five-to-eight shot list)
  11. Photo Project II: Shoot It
  12. Family Demo Day: mini gallery, reflect, choose what's next

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 10–14 who already loves taking pictures and is ready to get good at it. No photography background needed, and no prior AI experience needed. You lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and written out for you. Works with any phone camera; no special gear required.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily rattle off camera settings and jargon that a kid forgets by dinner, which teaches nothing about actually seeing a photo. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc built around light, framing, and the moment (not gear or apps), Socratic prompts that make the tutor ask what the learner notices before it names the technique, a guardrail that stops the tutor from composing the shot for them, and checkpoints that prove the eye is actually developing. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need a nice camera? No. Any phone camera works. This pack is about learning to see a photo, not about gear.
  • Do I need to know anything about photography myself? No. The AI tutor teaches the craft; you lead. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Which AI does it use? Works with any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, or similar. Free versions are enough; no paid plan needed. The prompts are written to work anywhere.
  • I've never used an AI chat assistant. Is this still for me? Yes. The guide's first section walks complete beginners through getting set up in about five minutes, then hands you the exact prompt to paste for every session.
  • Is my child's data collected? Not by us. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise every AI session yourself, under your own account, and the guide shows you what to keep out of the chat (and to describe, rather than upload, any photo that shows a child's face).
  • What ages? Roughly 10–14.
  • Refunds? Standard marketplace refund policy applies.
