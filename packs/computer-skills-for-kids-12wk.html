Most kids today can swipe and tap, but few have ever actually been taught to type without hunting and pecking, save a file somewhere they can find it again, search for something and judge whether it's true, or make something on screen instead of only watching. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding an 8–12-year-old from "where's the on button" to typing real sentences, organizing their own files, searching safely, and finishing a project they built themselves, using an AI tutor to talk them through every hands-on step while you do the leading. You do not need to be a computer expert.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly session guides, each built for one ~60-minute, fully scripted session: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint so you always know it landed, common mistakes to watch for, and practice.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes. Any free AI assistant works (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini), no paid plan needed.
  • A parent/teacher orientation: how to sit beside a learner using AI, why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor, and exactly what your one hour a week looks like.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when the learner is stuck or frustrated, bored or finding it too easy, when the AI starts doing the clicking itself, and to check real understanding, plus a tutor brief that keeps the AI talking your child through doing it themselves and a guardrail that keeps every example appropriate and every real click adult-approved.
  • Works wherever you live: the guide is written in US English, and every prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for spelling, units, money, school terms, and examples. Windows, Mac, and Chromebook all work.
  • A digital guide: no printer needed; read it on any screen.

The 12 weeks

  1. Meet Your Computer: its parts, and what each one does
  2. Turn It On and Get Around: logging in, opening apps, shutting down
  3. Touch Typing: Home Row
  4. Power Moves: Mouse, Clicks & Shortcuts
  5. Files & Folders: Where Things Live
  6. Searching Well and Safely
  7. Make It: A Document or Slideshow
  8. Drawing & Creating On Screen
  9. Staying Safe and Kind Online
  10. Project Week I: Plan It (a project that uses everything so far)
  11. Project Week II: Build It
  12. Demo Day: present, reflect, choose what's next

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 8–12 who has a laptop or desktop computer (Windows, Mac, or Chromebook) with a real keyboard and a mouse or trackpad available at home or school. No computer background needed, and no prior AI experience needed. You lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and written out for you, while the AI tutor walks your child through every hands-on step.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily click through a task, type the document, or run the search for your child, which teaches nothing. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that moves from the parts of a computer to touch typing to files to safe searching to actually making things (a document, a slideshow, digital art); a tutor that talks the learner through doing each step themselves instead of doing it for them; guardrails that keep every example age-appropriate and every real click adult-approved; and checkpoints that prove your child can do it, not just watch it done. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need to be a computer expert? No. The AI tutor explains and coaches the hands-on steps; you lead. Every session comes with a timed plan and the tutor instructions written in for you.
  • Which AI does it use? I've never used one. Any modern AI assistant works: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini. The free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. The guide opens with a "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" section that walks a complete beginner through setup in about five minutes.
  • Does my child need their own device? They need access to a real laptop or desktop computer with a keyboard and a mouse or trackpad during sessions (a phone or tablet won't do); typing, files, and searching are hands-on skills. A shared family computer is fine. No paid apps needed: a word processor, a slideshow app, a simple free paint program, and a web browser cover every week.
  • Is my child's data collected? Not by us. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise every AI session yourself, under your own account. The AI service you choose may store your chats, so the guide has you share only a first name and age, never an address, school, or other private details.
  • What ages? Roughly 8–12.
