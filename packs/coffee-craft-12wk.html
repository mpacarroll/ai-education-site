A masterclass you actually finish: the kind of coffee class a good roaster gives away "free" to sell you a subscription, except this one is built to make you genuinely good rather than to sell you the next bag. Most home coffee advice is a recipe: this teaches you the system underneath every good cup. Grind, ratio, water, temperature, time, and agitation aren't separate tips; they're six levers on one machine, and once you understand how they trade off, you can dial in any bean, on any method, by taste alone. Your AI tutor plays extraction coach: it has you taste first, predict what one change will do, then verify. Twelve weeks later you're not following someone else's recipe; you're reading your own cup and fixing it.

Who it's for

Adults who already love coffee and want to actually brew it well, not just buy better equipment and hope. Maybe you've followed a recipe card that stopped working the moment you switched beans, or you own three brewers and don't really know why one tastes better than another. No barista background required; a grinder (or pre-ground bag), hot water, and a willingness to taste slowly are the whole prerequisite.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

Ask a raw chatbot "how do I make better coffee?" and it hands you a recipe. Follow it once, switch beans, and you're lost again. This pack ships the system a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week progression through the six variables that actually control a cup, and a tutor set up to make you predict before you brew and diagnose by taste, not by someone else's numbers. You leave able to dial in a bag you've never seen before, on a machine you've never used, without opening a single recipe card.

  • Do I need expensive equipment? No. A grinder or a pre-ground bag, hot water, and any brewer works: French press, pour-over, drip, AeroPress, or espresso. The system applies to all of them.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Any modern AI assistant works (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini), and the free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. If you've never opened an AI chat assistant before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes before Week 1 begins.
  • Is this really a masterclass, or a course? It's a paid pack framed like a masterclass: the depth of paid training, self-paced, with an AI coach instead of a live instructor. We keep the framing honest: you finish this one.
  • Will this work with my favorite method already? Yes. Week 7 is specifically about mapping whatever method you already use onto the same six variables, not replacing it.
