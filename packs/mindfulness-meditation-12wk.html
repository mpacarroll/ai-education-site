Meditation isn't emptying your mind, feeling instantly calm, or outlasting a badge on an app; it's a trainable skill built from a few real mechanisms (attention, the wandering mind, non-judgmental awareness), and this 12-week plan teaches them one at a time, from first principles, with an AI tutor that asks what you noticed before it explains anything. Who it's for

Adults who've tried an app, kept a streak for nine days, and quietly stopped, and want a real practice instead: something they understand well enough to sustain on their own. No prior meditation, yoga, or Buddhist studies background assumed; if you can sit for two minutes and pay attention to your own breath, you're ready.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

Ask a raw chatbot to "teach me to meditate" and you'll get a generic script to read along to: useful once, forgettable the next day. This pack ships the structure and progression it lacks: a real mechanism-by-mechanism arc, checkpoints that make you actually sit rather than just read about sitting, and a tutor built to ask what you noticed instead of narrating a relaxation track at you. The difference between doing a guided meditation and building a practice that's yours.

Safety

This is educational, secular wellness content, not therapy, counseling, or medical/mental-health treatment, and the AI tutor is not a clinician. Meditation can occasionally surface difficult thoughts or feelings; the pack teaches you to notice and work with the ordinary kind, and to recognize when something is bigger than a solo sit and belongs with a qualified professional. Weeks that need it (the body scan, sitting with a difficult feeling) carry their own safety notes, and every prompt tells the AI to stop and point you to professional help or local emergency services if you describe real distress. If you have a history of trauma, panic attacks, or psychosis, or are in treatment for a mental-health condition, check with your clinician first.
