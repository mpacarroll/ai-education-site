The independence skills school doesn't teach. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding an 8–12-year-old through the everyday capability kids are rarely taught on purpose: staying organized, running a morning and bedtime routine, helping around the house, keeping their own space tidy, basic laundry, packing their own bag, being on time, and solving a small problem themselves, with an AI tutor that coaches while you stay the leader. No money topics here, and no expertise required from you.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each built to run in one ~60-minute sitting: a plain-language concept explanation, a concrete worked example, a timed session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in (nothing to write yourself), a "show me" checkpoint (a real moment your child demonstrates the skill to you), common mistakes to watch for, and practice to lock it in.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes about five minutes: it walks a complete beginner from zero to ready, using a free AI assistant (no paid plan needed), plus a parent/teacher orientation on how to sit beside a learner using AI and why the adult stays the leader while the AI is the tutor.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when the learner is frustrated or bored, when the AI starts handing over a finished checklist, and to check the skill is real. Paste and go.
  • Every step is fully scripted and timed, so you're never guessing what to say or do next, with no expertise needed from you.

The 12 weeks

  1. Your Own Command Center: a "home" for everything, so nothing gets lost
  2. Own Your Morning: a routine the learner runs themselves
  3. Own Your Bedtime: winding down and getting ready for tomorrow
  4. Helping the Team: one real, regular chore, owned start to finish
  5. Keeping Your Space Tidy: a fast, repeatable system
  6. Basic Laundry (With the Adult): sort, help wash, and fold
  7. Packing Your Own Bag: complete and ready, checked twice
  8. Being on Time: working backward from a leave-time
  9. Solving a Small Problem Yourself: a simple process before asking for help
  10. Project Week I: Plan a Big Kid Day (a real, hour-by-hour plan)
  11. Project Week II: Run the Big Kid Day (the real thing, for real)
  12. Family Demo Day: present the story, celebrate growth, choose what's next

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 8–12 who's ready for more responsibility and more pride in handling it. No special expertise needed. You lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and written out for you, and the AI tutor does the coaching.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily tell your child exactly what to do, one instruction at a time, which builds compliance, not capability. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that moves from small systems to a full independent day, Socratic prompts that make the tutor coach instead of direct, guardrails that keep every task age-appropriate and adult-supervised where it matters (laundry, appliances), and checkpoints that prove the skill lives with your child, not the chat window. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see real-world progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need to be super organized myself? No. The AI tutor does the coaching; you lead by showing up and asking for the checkpoint. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Works with any modern AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini), and the free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. The guide is written in US English, and every prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for spelling, units, and examples. If you've never used an AI chat assistant before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes, start to finish.
  • Is my child's data collected? Not by us. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult, with no login and no child account. You start and supervise every AI session yourself, under your own account. The AI provider may store those chats, so the guide asks you to share only a first name and age, never anything private.
  • Is this safe around laundry and appliances? Yes. The laundry week is explicitly adult-run: you always operate the washer and dryer settings and keep the detergent (pods most of all) while your child sorts, loads alongside you, and folds. The chore, tidying, and laundry prompts tell the AI never to have your child use appliances, sharp tools, or cleaning chemicals alone, and to keep every suggestion age-appropriate.
  • What ages? Roughly 8–12.
