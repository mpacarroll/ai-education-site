Who it's for

Adults earning their high-school equivalency who want a plan, not just a pile of practice questions - whether this is your first attempt at any subject or you're retaking one and need to know exactly where the remaining points are.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A free chatbot will answer whatever question you paste in, which feels productive but skips the part that actually raises scores: knowing where your real gaps are, practicing under the clock, and reviewing every miss instead of moving on. This pack ships the structure a raw chatbot lacks - an adaptive diagnosis that sorts every subject into solid/shaky/missing, a real 12-week progression from drill to full timed practice, and a tutor that reviews the why behind every miss instead of just marking it wrong. A prep book can't adapt to your gaps; this can.

FAQ

  • Is this official GED material? No. This pack is not affiliated with or endorsed by GED Testing Service, LLC or any test maker, and it contains no official test content. Use it alongside official GED materials (GED.com, GED Ready practice tests) and the current official format, which is the source of truth for content and rules.
  • Will this guarantee I pass? No. No pack, book, or tutor can guarantee a score or a passing outcome. This plan gives your effort real structure - diagnose, drill, time, review - and honest score tracking, not a promise.
  • I'm not in the US. Does it still work? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every prompt tells the AI tutor to use your country or region for spelling, units, money, and examples. The guide also reminds you to confirm on GED.com that the GED is offered where you live and is the credential you need.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Works with any modern AI assistant - ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini - and the free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan required. If you've never opened an AI chat before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes, so you're ready before Week 1 starts.
  • Do I need to take all four subjects at once? No - the GED lets you schedule subjects independently, and Week 12 helps you build an honest, subject-by-subject scheduling plan based on your real scores.
  • Do I need to already be strong in all four subjects? No - Week 1 is a full diagnostic that finds your real starting point, and the middle weeks drill exactly what's shaky or missing in each subject.
  • What if my score plateaus? The guide includes a plateau prompt: the tutor looks at your actual error pattern and changes the approach instead of just repeating the same drills.
  • Does it cover the Extended Response essay? Yes - Week 4 is dedicated to planning and writing it under timed conditions, and it's part of any full timed RLA practice in weeks 9, 11, and 12.
