Who it's for

Adults who use the internet constantly (for work, for everything) but have never had anyone explain what's actually happening underneath. No technical background assumed, no math, no coding. If you've ever wondered why your wifi is slow, what that padlock icon means, or where "the cloud" actually is, you're the audience.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will answer "what is DNS?" with a paragraph you'll forget by lunch. This pack ships the structure it lacks: a real 12-week progression where each idea builds on the last, a tutor set up to make you restate every concept in plain language before moving on, and a project that has you trace and diagnose your OWN connection instead of memorizing an abstract diagram.
