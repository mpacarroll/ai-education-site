Short description (above the fold)

A masterclass you actually finish — on the eight senses you've been using every second of your life without ever being taught how they work. This is not a trivia list of eye and ear facts. It's 12 weeks of understanding perception as your brain's best-guess construction of the world, not a camera or microphone recording it — so illusions stop feeling like magic tricks and start feeling like evidence you can explain. Each week an AI tutor walks you through the mechanism, then hands you a real illusion or a mini-test and makes you predict, try it, and explain the "why" yourself. No biology background needed.

Format: A digital guide with 12 ready-to-run, fully scripted sessions — you lead one ~60-minute session each week with an AI tutor as your perception coach. Every step is timed and every AI prompt is written for you in advance; no biology background or prior AI experience needed.

FAQ

  • Do I need any biology or neuroscience background? None. Every sense starts from what you already notice about your own body and is built up in plain language with everyday warm-ups.
  • Is this just a list of cool illusions? No — illusions are the evidence, not the point. Each one is used to prove how the underlying construction process works, and you have to explain the mechanism, not just enjoy the trick.
  • Which AI does it use? I've never used one. Any modern AI chat assistant works — ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini — and the free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes before Week 1 starts.
  • Do I need any special equipment? No. Every warm-up uses things you already have - paper, a mirror, your own hands, or common food you can pinch your nose while eating.
  • Is this a substitute for seeing a doctor about my eyes, ears, or a real symptom? No. This is educational content about how healthy perception normally works, not diagnosis or treatment - if something feels genuinely wrong, that's a question for a licensed professional, not this course.
