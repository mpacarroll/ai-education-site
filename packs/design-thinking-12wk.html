What you get

Who it's for

Parents and teachers guiding a learner aged roughly 10–17 who likes making things (art, crafts, games, or apps). Medium-agnostic and no design background needed on your part; the tutor critiques, you keep the learner in the driver's seat. You lead one ~60-minute session each week, fully scripted, with every step timed and written out for you, so no expertise or prior AI experience is needed.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A chatbot will spit out a "finished" design and rob the learner of the actual skill: noticing, testing, iterating. This pack keeps the learner's taste in charge and uses the AI as critic and stand-in user, with a 12-week arc that ends in a real deliverable and a portfolio crit. Process over output, which is the whole point of design.

  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini. Free versions are enough; no paid plan needed. Never used an AI chat assistant before? The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes.
