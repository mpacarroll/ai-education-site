Safety

This is educational content, not medical or professional coaching advice. Talk to a physician before starting a new exercise program, learn each movement's form carefully — ideally with a qualified coach watching in person — and stop immediately at sharp or joint pain; normal muscular effort and mild soreness are expected, pain is not.
