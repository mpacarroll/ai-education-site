A masterclass you actually finish. Most people who try to learn programming end up pasting code they can't read. This 12-week plan does the opposite. You pair-program with an AI tutor that codes alongside you but refuses to let you keep a single line you can't explain. Format: a digital guide with twelve ready-to-run weekly sessions. You lead one ~60-minute session each week, and every session is fully scripted: every step timed and written out for you. No programming background or AI experience needed.

Who it's for

Adult beginners who want to really learn to program (career-curious, or just wanting to automate their own work) with no computer science background assumed. If you have a laptop or desktop computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux), can open a browser, and are willing to write a little code yourself, you're ready. Week 1 walks you through installing free tools from their official sites.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily write your whole program and leave you unable to read it; that's how people end up "vibe-coding" apps they can't fix or explain. This pack ships the structure and discipline it lacks: a real 12-week progression from your first line to a working tool, and a tutor set up to be Socratic and mastery-gated: it makes you predict, read back, and explain every line before it moves on, and it stops you when you're copying. Understanding that compounds, not code that evaporates.

  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI chat assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, or similar. Free versions are enough; no paid plan needed. If you've never used one before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes.
  • Which language? Python, one of the most popular first languages and widely used for automating everyday work. The thinking transfers to any language after.
  • Is this really a masterclass, or a paid pack? It's an honest paid pack framed the way a masterclass is: self-paced, structured, and coached by your AI tutor. The difference from a free video course is that it makes you actually do the work and can tell when you're not.
  • Will it just write my code for me? Deliberately not. The whole point is that you understand every line. The tutor pair-programs but won't let you ship what you can't explain.
