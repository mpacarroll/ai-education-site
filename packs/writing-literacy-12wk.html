Write Like You Mean It — A 12-Week Guide to Raising a Strong Writer With an AI Tutor.

Short description (above the fold)

In the AI era, clear writing is the master skill — it's how you think, how you persuade, and now how you direct machines. This 12-week plan uses an AI tutor as an editor, never a ghostwriter: every week the learner writes something by their own hand first, then improves it with the tutor. That one rule is the whole curriculum, and this pack protects it for you.

What you get

  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes — any free AI assistant works, no paid plan needed — plus a parent/teacher orientation on the one rule that matters: AI-as-editor, never AI-as-ghostwriter.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter, ready for the frustrated, the bored, the stuck, and the rushed session.
  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each a ready-to-run ~60-minute plan with: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan, copy/paste AI-tutor prompts at every step (with the "respond and question, don't rewrite" guardrail built in), a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, and independent practice.
  • A digital guide — no login, no app, no child account.

Who it's for

Parents and teachers guiding a learner aged roughly 10–16 — and equally useful for adults who avoid writing. No English-teacher background needed, and no prior AI experience needed; the five-minute onboarding gets a complete beginner set up, the tutor coaches, and you protect the one rule.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

Hand a kid a chatbot and you get a ghostwriter — and a writer who never develops. This pack flips it: the tutor responds, questions, and edits with the learner, never for them, and the guardrails hold that line. Structured weekly craft plus a real anti-plagiarism habit, which is exactly what a raw chatbot won't give you.

FAQ

  • Won't the AI just write it for them? Not the way this pack runs it. Every session's prompt tells the tutor to edit and question, never to draft — and the guardrail enforces it.
  • Do I need to be a strong writer? No. You lead and protect the "own hand first" rule; the tutor does the coaching.
  • Which AI does it use? I've never used one. Any modern AI chat assistant works — ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini — and the free version of any of them is enough. The guide's first section walks a complete beginner through setup in about five minutes.
  • Is my child's data collected? No. It's a digital guide sold to you, the adult — no login, no child account. You start and supervise sessions under your own account.
