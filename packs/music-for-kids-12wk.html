Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 8–12 who wants to understand music, not just take lessons on one instrument. Works with any instrument you already have, a simple keyboard or piano app, or just a voice and a pair of hands. No musical background needed — you lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and every AI prompt written for you, and just need to be a good set of ears.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily generate a finished melody or a set of lyrics on request — which skips the actual skill and leaves your child unable to make the next one themselves. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that builds the ear first (beat, pitch, dynamics, tempo, mood) before it ever asks for a finished piece, Socratic prompts that make the tutor ask what the child hears instead of naming it for them, a guardrail that stops it from composing or playing on the learner's behalf, and checkpoints that prove your child can make music, not just request it. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.
