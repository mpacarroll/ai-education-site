Make Music

Turn "can I play something?" into real musical understanding. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding an 8–12-year-old from feeling a steady beat all the way to performing an original tune they wrote themselves, using an AI tutor that teaches why music sounds the way it does (beat, high and low, loud and soft, happy and sad) by ear first, using any instrument or a simple app you already have. You do not need a musical background.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly session guides, each built for one fully scripted ~60-minute session you lead: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor instructions built in, a "show me" checkpoint (almost always your child playing or singing something out loud), common mistakes to watch for, and practice. Every step is timed and written out for you; no musical or AI expertise needed.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes, using a free AI assistant; no paid plan needed. Plus a parent/teacher orientation on why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor (an ear and a question-asker, never a performer).
  • An AI-tutor playbook built into every session plan: a launch prompt that opens each session, plus four rescue prompts: one for a frustrated or stuck learner, one for a bored learner, a "never play or compose it for them" reset for when the AI starts handing over answers, and a check that your child really understands.
  • Worked examples and checkpoints at every step, in place of generic rubrics, so wins are visible and momentum is easy to keep.

The 12 weeks

  1. Finding the Beat: feeling and keeping a steady beat
  2. High and Low
  3. Loud and Soft
  4. Fast and Slow
  5. Happy and Sad Sounds
  6. Patterns and Repetition: build a rhythm of your own
  7. Your First Simple Melody
  8. Putting Sounds Together: layering a beat and a melody
  9. Making Your Own Tune: an original composition
  10. Project Week I: Plan It (the piece for Demo Day)
  11. Project Week II: Build It
  12. Family Demo Day: perform, reflect, choose what's next

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 8–12 who wants to understand music, not just take lessons on one instrument. Works with any instrument you already have, a simple keyboard or piano app, or just a voice and a pair of hands. No musical background needed. You lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and written out for you, and just need to be a good set of ears.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily generate a finished melody or a set of lyrics on request, which skips the actual skill and leaves your child unable to make the next one themselves. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that builds the ear first (beat, pitch, dynamics, tempo, mood) before it ever asks for a finished piece, Socratic prompts that make the tutor ask what the child hears instead of naming it for them, a guardrail that stops it from composing or playing on the learner's behalf, and checkpoints that prove your child can make music, not just request it. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need a musical background? No. The AI tutor coaches by ear; you lead by showing up and asking for the checkpoint. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • What instrument or app do we need? Any one thing that makes sound: a real instrument you already have, a free keyboard or piano app on a phone or tablet, a xylophone, or even just your child's own voice and hands. No specific gear or brand required.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Any modern AI assistant works (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini), and the free version of any of them is enough. If you've never used an AI chat assistant before, the guide's first section walks you through setup from scratch in about five minutes, no experience required.
  • Is my child's data collected? Not by us. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult, with no login and no child account. You start and supervise every AI session yourself, under your own account, so your AI provider's privacy policy applies; the guide shows you how to share no more than a first name and age.
  • What ages? Roughly 8–12.
  • Refunds? Standard marketplace refund policy applies.
