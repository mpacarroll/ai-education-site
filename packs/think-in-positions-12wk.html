Everything in here you could technically piece together from a free chatbot and a chessboard; this pack is the masterclass that actually gets you to finish it. Games (chess, cards, Go, whatever you already play) become the training ground for a real mental model of strategy: position, tempo, evaluation, trade-offs, and thinking several moves ahead. You don't just get better at a game; you learn to read any situation the same way, and by the end you apply the whole method to one real decision in your own life.

Format: a digital guide with 12 ready-to-run ~60-minute sessions. You lead one session a week; every step is scripted, timed and written out for you. No strategy-game expertise or prior AI experience needed.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each ~60 minutes and fully scripted: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, and practice.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes. No paid plan needed; any free AI assistant works.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when you're stuck, when the AI starts handing you answers, when it feels too easy, and to check you really understand, so you're never left improvising.
  • An AI-tutor playbook: a Socratic starter that questions before it tells, a rescue prompt for when the branches get overwhelming, a "level me up" prompt for when it feels easy, and a guardrail that keeps you reasoning instead of just being handed the answer.
  • A two-part real-world project that takes an actual decision in your life through the full method (mapped, planned, and made), plus a one-page personal strategy checklist you build in the final week.

Who it's for

Adults who want to think more strategically at work and in life, and are willing to use a game they already enjoy (chess, cards, Go, checkers, even a strategy video game) as the training ground. No competitive-player background required; you just need to be willing to play something and then explain your reasoning out loud.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will just tell you the "best move" the moment you ask, which teaches you nothing you can reuse tomorrow. This pack ships the structure a free chatbot lacks: a real 12-week progression from first principles to a real decision, a tutor deliberately set up to question you before it answers, and a method (position, tempo, evaluation, trade-offs, thinking ahead) that transfers to situations no chatbot tip-list ever covers. You leave able to generate the analysis yourself, not just remember a list of tips.

FAQ

  • Do I need any experience? None with strategy games specifically. Week 1 starts by defining strategy itself, using whatever game you already enjoy playing.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI chat assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini. Free versions are enough; no paid plan required. Never used one before? The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes.
  • Do I need to be good at the game to benefit? No, the game is the training ground, not the point. You're learning to read positions and trade-offs, not to win tournaments.
  • What's the real-world project? In weeks 10-11 you apply the whole method to one actual decision in your life (mapping the position, then making the move), with the tutor stress- testing your plan before you act.
