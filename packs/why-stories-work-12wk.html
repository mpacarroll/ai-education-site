Who it's for

Adults who want to tell, write, and pitch stories that land: at work, on stage, and in life. The person giving the toast, the founder pitching the room, the manager who needs the update to stick, anyone who has ever watched eyes glaze and wished they knew why. No writing background assumed; you only need something worth saying.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

Ask a raw chatbot to "make my story better" and it will quietly rewrite it in beige AI prose and hand it back, and the skill never transfers. This pack ships the structure and discipline it lacks: a real 12-week progression through the working parts of story, a tutor set up to diagnose the mechanism and make YOU rebuild it, and a coach that will play the audience and tell you honestly where it drifted. You leave able to build and fix stories yourself, not holding one paragraph the machine wrote once.

FAQ

  • Do I need any writing experience? None. Week 1 is noticing why a story you already love hooked you. Every week is about seeing and building the parts, not fancy prose.
  • Is this only for writers? No. It's aimed as much at pitches, toasts, and talks as at the page; the same machine drives all of them, which is the whole point.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Any modern AI chat assistant works: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini. The free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. Never used one before? The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes, then hands you the exact prompts for every session.
  • Will the AI just write my stories? Deliberately not. The tutor is set up to coach and to play the audience, never to ghostwrite, because the skill only sticks if you build the story yourself.
  • How is this different from a business-writing course? That's about clarity: getting a fast yes. This is about pull: making people lean in and remember. Different machine, different goal.
  • Does it work outside the US? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every prompt tells the AI to use your country or region (the one you give it, or one it infers from how you write, or it asks) for spelling, units, money, and examples.
  • Do I have to share painful or private stories? No. You choose every story, a small everyday one teaches the same craft, and the guide shows you how to leave out names and private details.
