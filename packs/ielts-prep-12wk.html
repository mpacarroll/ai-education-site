FAQ

  • Is this an official IELTS product? No. This pack is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the IELTS test makers (the British Council, IDP IELTS, or Cambridge University Press & Assessment) or any test center, and it contains no official test content. Use it alongside the official IELTS practice tests and preparation materials as your source of truth.
  • Will this guarantee a certain band score or a visa/admission outcome? No. No band score, test result, or admission, visa, or migration outcome is guaranteed. This pack is a study system that puts your effort where it counts; the result depends on you, your starting point, and how much you practice.
  • Which AI does it use? I've never used one before. Any modern AI chat assistant works (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini), and the free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. A version with voice or audio input helps for the Speaking and Listening weeks, but isn't required. If you've never opened an AI chat assistant before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes, so you're ready before Week 1 starts.
  • Do I need to already be strong in English? No. Week 1 is a diagnostic specifically so the plan meets you where you actually are across all four sections, not where you assume you are.
  • How much time per week? One ~60-minute guided session, once a week, fully scripted: every step is timed and written out for you, so you're never guessing what to do or say next. Weeks 9-12 also include a full timed practice test outside that hour, from about 15 minutes for the Speaking mock to about 2 hours 45 minutes for the final dress rehearsal.
  • Is it up to date with the current test? It teaches the current computer-delivered IELTS (paper-based IELTS ended in mid-2026), including the optional handwritten "Writing on Paper" where test centers offer it. Formats can change, so check the official IELTS site for your country before test day.
  • Will it fit where I live? The guide is written in US English, and every prompt tells the AI to use your country or region (given, inferred, or asked) for spelling, units, money, and examples. IELTS itself accepts British or American spelling, as long as you're consistent.
