Grammar and Spelling That Stick

Worksheets don't make grammar stick; understanding why a rule exists does. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding an 8–12-year-old from shaky sentences to a clean, self-edited paragraph (sentence structure, punctuation, spelling patterns, and the mistakes that trip up almost every kid), using an AI tutor that explains the pattern and gives targeted, low-stakes practice on the learner's own writing, while you do the leading. You do not need to remember your own grammar rules.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each fully scripted for about 60 minutes: a plain-language concept explanation, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint so you always know it landed, common mistakes to watch for, and practice.
  • "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here": a five-minute onboarding in the front matter that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready, using a free AI assistant (no paid plan needed).
  • A parent/teacher orientation covering how to sit beside a learner using AI, why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor, and exactly what your one ~60-minute session a week looks like.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when the learner is frustrated or bored, when the AI starts handing over fixed sentences, and for checking the learner really understands.
  • A digital guide you read on any device, no printing required.

The 12 weeks

  1. Meet Your Grammar & Spelling Tutor: a first gentle look at how they write now
  2. Sentences That Work: complete sentences vs. fragments and run-ons
  3. Nouns, Verbs & the Sentence Core
  4. Punctuation That Helps the Reader
  5. Spelling Patterns & Rules: not memorizing word by word
  6. Tricky Words & Why They Trip Us: homophones and a personal watch-list
  7. Capitals & the Basics
  8. Fixing the Most Common Mistakes: a personal top-three list
  9. Editing Your Own Writing: a repeatable edit pass
  10. Project Week I: Plan Your Clean Paragraph
  11. Project Week II: Write and Edit It
  12. Demo Day: Show Your Clean Paragraph (and pick the next goal)

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged 8–12. No grammar-expert background needed, and no prior AI experience needed; you lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and written out for you. Works whether your learner mixes up their/there, writes run-on sentences, or just finds grammar worksheets deadly boring.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily fix your learner's sentence and move on, which teaches nothing. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc from sentence basics to a self-edited paragraph, Socratic prompts that make the tutor explain the pattern before it corrects anything, guardrails that stop it from just handing over a fixed sentence, and checkpoints that prove the learner can catch and fix it themselves. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need to remember my own grammar rules? No. The AI tutor explains the pattern; you lead. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Works with any modern AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini); free versions are enough, no paid plan needed. If you've never used an AI chat assistant before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes.
  • Is my child's data collected? No. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise each session under your own AI account, and the guide tells you what not to paste into a chat.
  • My kid thinks grammar is boring. Will this help? That's exactly what it's built for. The tutor teaches through the learner's own sentences and real interests, not worksheets, and the bored-rescue prompt is built in for the days it still feels dry.
  • We use UK (or Canadian, Australian) spelling. Will it mark my learner wrong? No. The guide is written in US English, but every week's launch prompt tells the AI to use your country or region (as you give it, as it infers from how you write, or by asking) and hold your learner to that place's spelling.
  • What ages? Roughly 8–12. Each week's launch prompt includes your learner's age, so the tutor pitches its explanations to them.
