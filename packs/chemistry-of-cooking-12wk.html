Safety

This is educational content, not professional advice, and it's about food chemistry only. A real kitchen means sharp knives, high heat, hot oil, open flame, raw proteins, and allergies — keep a smoke alarm and extinguisher within reach, watch hot oil, cook proteins through, and check for allergens every time. Never mix household cleaning chemicals in the name of an "experiment"; keep cleaning products out of the cooking entirely. Stop when unsure.
