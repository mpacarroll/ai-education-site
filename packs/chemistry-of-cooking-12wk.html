Who it's for

Adults who can already cook a little and are tired of being chained to recipes. You want to understand why: why the pan has to be dry to brown, why the sauce broke, why a squeeze of lemon fixes a flat dish, so you can open the fridge and build something real from what's there. Curiosity required; a chemistry degree is not.

Ask a raw chatbot "what can I make?" and it spits out a recipe; you're following again, none the wiser. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week progression from levers to reactions to whole-dish design, and a tutor set up to teach the mechanism, make you predict before you test, and then reconcile the gap, which is where intuition is actually built. Safety

This is educational content, not professional advice, and it's about food chemistry only. A real kitchen means sharp knives, high heat, hot oil, open flame, raw proteins, and allergies. Keep a smoke alarm and extinguisher within reach, watch hot oil, cook proteins to safe temperatures, and check for allergens every time. The raw-fish and raw-egg steps are optional, with no-fish and pasteurized-egg alternatives, and are flagged as unsuitable for anyone pregnant, very young, older, or with a weakened immune system. Never mix household cleaning chemicals in the name of an "experiment"; keep cleaning products out of the cooking entirely. Stop when unsure.
