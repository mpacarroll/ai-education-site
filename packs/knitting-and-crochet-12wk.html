Short description (above the fold)

Most "learn to knit" content teaches you to follow one pattern. This 12-week plan teaches you the fundamentals underneath every pattern — the core stitches in both knitting and crochet, how to read any pattern's shorthand, and how to fix the mistakes that make beginners quit — so that by week 12 you're not just holding one finished scarf, you can pick up any beginner pattern and make it. No experience assumed, and your AI tutor walks every stitch with you since it can't watch your hands — it teaches by asking what you see and feel, and has you narrate your motions back.

Adults who want to make real things with yarn — no experience assumed. You lead one ~60-minute session each week, fully scripted, with every step timed and every AI prompt written for you, so no expertise is needed going in. Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will describe a stitch once and move on, leaving you to guess whether what's in your hands is actually right. This pack ships the structure it lacks: a real stitch-by-stitch progression, swatch-based checkpoints before you're allowed to advance, and a tutor set up to ask what your yarn and needles are doing rather than assume it can see them. You finish not with one memorized pattern, but with the fundamentals to read and make any beginner pattern you find.

Safety

This is educational content, not professional instruction. Knitting needles, crochet hooks, and the scissors you'll use for trimming yarn are pointed and sharp — handle them with care, keep them away from young children and pets, and take breaks if your hands or wrists start to ache.
