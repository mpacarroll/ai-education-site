See and Draw

Ages 8-12 · Arts and making

Take the free placement check first: 15 minutes, and it names the week to start at.

Turn "I can't draw" into "look what I made." This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding an 8–12-year-old from their first two-minute sketch to a finished drawing they present to the family, using an AI tutor that coaches from a photo of their drawing while you do the leading. The secret this pack teaches: drawing is not a talent, it's a skill of seeing: real edges, real shapes, and the real spaces between things. Any kid who can look closely can learn to draw what's in front of them. You do not need to be able to draw yourself.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each built to run for about 60 minutes and fully scripted: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint so you always know it landed, common mistakes to watch for, and practice to lock it in.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes. No account experience assumed, and a free AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini) is enough.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for the moments every parent hits: the learner is stuck or frustrated, the AI starts describing or drawing the answer, the learner is bored, or you want to check they really understand.
  • A digital guide: you lead one ~60-minute session a week, and every step is written out for you in advance. No drawing background or AI experience needed.

The 12 weeks

  1. Anyone Can Learn to See: a baseline drawing to grow from
  2. Loosening Up: Marks, Lines, and Confidence
  3. Drawing the Edges
  4. Shapes Hiding in Everything
  5. The Spaces Between Things: negative space
  6. Looking Closely: Slow Drawing
  7. Light and Shadow
  8. Drawing From Real Life: A Still Life
  9. Adding Your Own Style
  10. Project Week I: Plan It (choose a subject, sketch thumbnails)
  11. Project Week II: Draw It
  12. Family Demo Day: present, reflect, choose what's next

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 8–12 who says "I can't draw" or just wants to get better. No drawing background needed, and you don't need to have used an AI assistant before. Your job is one fully scripted ~60-minute session, a photo, and encouragement each week.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily generate a picture for your learner or describe exactly what to draw, which teaches nothing about seeing. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc built on how artists actually learn (edges, shapes, negative space, slow looking, light and shadow), a Socratic playbook that has the tutor coach from a photo of the real drawing and ask before it tells, a guardrail that stops it from ever drawing the answer, and checkpoints that prove the seeing skill actually landed. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need to be able to draw myself? No. The AI tutor coaches by looking at photos of your learner's drawings and asking questions; you lead by showing up with paper and a camera. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Any modern AI assistant with image understanding: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini. Free versions are enough; no paid plan required. The guide's first section, "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here," walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes, and every session gives you the exact prompt to paste.
  • Is my child's data collected? Not by us. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise every AI session yourself, under your own account, including any time a drawing photo is shared with the tutor. The AI provider you choose may store those chats under its own privacy policy, so the guide shows you how to photograph just the paper, with no faces or anything that shows where you live, and to share only a first name and age.
  • What ages? Roughly 8–12.
  • Does it work outside the US? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for spelling, units, money, school terms, and familiar everyday words.
  • Refunds? Standard marketplace refund policy applies.
