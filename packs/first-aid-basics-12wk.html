Most people freeze in an emergency not because they're weak, but because they've never practiced the decisions. This 12-week plan turns an AI tutor into a calm scenario partner: it walks you through Assess-Call-Act until it's automatic, quizzes you on real situations, and teaches you the common first-aid basics, while relentlessly pointing you to your local emergency number and to certified hands-on training for the physical skills, like CPR, that only real practice can teach.

What you get

This is a deep, ready-to-run digital guide, not a tip sheet. Who it's for

Adults who want the knowledge and composure to help in an emergency until professionals arrive: parents, caregivers, coworkers, neighbors. No medical background needed; every skill starts from zero and builds toward calm, correct decisions under pressure.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily improvise medical advice with total confidence, exactly what you don't want in a crisis. This pack ships the structure and guardrails it lacks: a real 12-week progression built on one decision loop (Assess-Call-Act), scenario drills instead of a tip list, and a tutor set up to never let you skip the call for help and to route you to certified hands-on training for physical skills like CPR. Composure with a safety net, not confident guessing.

Safety

This pack is educational only and is not a substitute for certified first-aid/CPR training or professional medical care. It teaches recognition, decision-making, and the common first-aid basics, and just as importantly, when to stop and call. In any real emergency, call your local emergency number immediately; do not let this pack or any AI tutor delay that call.
