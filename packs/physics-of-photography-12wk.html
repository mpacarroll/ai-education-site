A masterclass you actually finish, the one that teaches you to think in light instead of hunting for the right preset. Presets and "Auto" modes hide the physics: aperture is a literal opening in glass, shutter speed is how long you let light in, ISO is how hard you amplify what little light you have, and a lens's focal length quietly reshapes the space in your frame. This 12-week masterclass teaches that physics from the ground up (how light behaves, how a lens bends it, how a sensor records it) so that in any light, indoors or out, sunset or midnight, you can reason your way to the shot instead of guessing at settings someone else optimized for a different scene.

Format: a digital guide with 12 ready-to-run weekly sessions, about 60 minutes each, fully scripted. You lead the session; an AI photography coach reviews your actual shots. No photography or AI experience needed.

Who it's for

Adults who own a camera or a phone and want to understand light and optics so they can make, not luck into, a great photo. No photography experience assumed, no AI chat experience assumed, and no expensive gear required beyond a camera or phone with a manual or "Pro" mode. The guide is written in US English, and every prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for spelling, units, and examples, so it works wherever you live.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

Ask a raw chatbot "what settings should I use" and it hands you a number to copy, the exact habit that leaves you helpless the moment the light changes. This pack ships the method a chatbot lacks: a real progression through the physics of light, lenses, and sensors, and a tutor set up to make you reason from the light in front of you before it ever confirms a setting. You leave able to reason your way to the shot in light you've never shot in before, not able to repeat one more preset.

FAQ

  • Do I need a fancy camera? No. Any camera or phone with a manual or "Pro" mode is enough; this is about how you think, not what you buy. Most phones have a fixed aperture, so the aperture week gives phone users a path that works with one shot and the stop math.
  • Do I need any experience? None, with photography or with AI. Week 1 starts with simply watching how light changes before a camera ever comes out.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI chat assistant with photo input: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini. Free versions are enough; no paid plan needed (free plans may limit how many photos you can upload, sometimes to a few a day, and describing a shot in words works too). If you've never used an AI chat assistant before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes. You show it your shots and your reasoning; the prompts work anywhere, no special software.
  • Will it teach me editing or Photoshop? No. This teaches you to get the shot right in-camera through physics. Editing and RAW processing are flagged as a great next step in week 12.
  • Will it just tell me the right settings? No, that's the point. The tutor makes you reason from the light first and only confirms or corrects your guess.
