A masterclass you actually finish — the one that teaches you to think in light instead of hunting for the right preset. Presets and "Auto" modes hide the physics: aperture is a literal opening in glass, shutter speed is how long you let light in, ISO is how hard you amplify what little light you have, and a lens's focal length quietly reshapes the space in your frame.
