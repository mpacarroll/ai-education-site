FAQ

  • Is this official ISEE or SSAT content? No. This pack is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to E3n (the organization that now administers both the ISEE and the SSAT, formed by the merger of the Educational Records Bureau and the Enrollment Management Association), or any other test maker, and it contains no official or leaked test questions. Use it alongside official practice materials for your specific exam (the official ISEE or SSAT guides and practice tests) as your source of truth for real questions and the current format.
  • Will this guarantee a certain score? No. No pack, book, or tutor can guarantee a score or an admission outcome, and this one won't claim to. What it offers is an honest diagnostic, focused practice on your actual gaps, and real timed review; the result depends on the work you put in.
  • Does this cover both the ISEE and the SSAT? Yes. They are still separate exams, and between them they cover the five section types this pack drills (Verbal Reasoning, Quantitative Reasoning, Reading, Math Achievement, and an unscored Essay), but they don't share all five: the SSAT has no separate Math Achievement section (SSAT students use their Quantitative sections instead), and only the SSAT tests analogies (the ISEE uses sentence completions). The ISEE Primary Levels (applying to grades 2 to 4) test Reading and Mathematics (Primary 2 adds a short listening section), with no essay, so a Primary student skips the Verbal and essay weeks and uses the math weeks as math practice. Level names, timing, and scoring/guessing rules also differ between them and by level. Week 2 has the student look up and confirm the exact rules for their specific exam and level before drilling begins.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI chat assistant works with these prompts (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini), and a free version of any of them is enough. Never used one before? The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes.
  • Do I need to already know my score or which test I'm taking? No. Week 1 is the diagnostic and week 2 has you look up and understand your exam's format. If you haven't chosen between the ISEE and SSAT yet, the free placement check walks through it, starting with what each target school requires. You start wherever you actually are, not where you assume you are.
  • What if my score plateaus? The rescue prompts include a plateau prompt built for exactly this: it changes the approach and targets the specific error pattern instead of repeating the same drills.
