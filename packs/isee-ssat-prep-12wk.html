Use it alongside official practice materials for your specific exam (ERB's official ISEE guides and practice tests, or the SSATB's official SSAT guides and practice tests) as your source of truth for real questions and the current format.
  • Will this guarantee a certain score? No. No pack, book, or tutor can guarantee a score or an admission outcome — this one won't claim to. What it offers is an honest diagnostic, focused practice on your actual gaps, and real timed review; the result depends on the work you put in.
  • Does this cover both the ISEE and the SSAT? Yes — the two exams share the same five section types this pack drills (Verbal Reasoning, Quantitative Reasoning, Reading, Math Achievement, and an unscored Essay), even though level names, timing, and scoring/guessing rules differ between them and by level. Week 2 has the student look up and confirm the exact rules for their specific exam and level before drilling begins.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI chat assistant works with these prompts — ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini — and a free version of any of them is enough. Never used one before? The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes.
  • Do I need to already know my score or which test I'm taking? No — week 1 is the diagnostic and week 2 covers choosing and understanding the specific exam. You start wherever you actually are, not where you assume you are.
  • What if my score plateaus? The playbook includes a plateau prompt built for exactly this: it changes the approach and targets the specific error pattern instead of repeating the same drills.
