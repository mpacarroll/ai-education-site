The ground, the ocean, the weather, the seasons, and the night sky are all one connected story, but most kids just never get to see the whole thing at once. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding a 9–13-year-old from "what's under my feet?" to naming the planets and reading the night sky, using an AI tutor to ask, explain, and adapt while you do the leading. You do not need an earth-science or astronomy background.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each built to run in about 60 minutes and fully scripted: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint so you always know it landed, common mistakes to watch for, and practice to lock it in.
  • "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here": a five-minute onboarding in the front matter that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready, using a free AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini). No paid plan needed, and no prior AI experience assumed.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for the moments every guide needs: the learner is frustrated or stuck, the AI explains before the learner has guessed, the learner is bored, or you want to check they really understand.
  • You lead one ~60-minute session a week: every step is timed and written out for you in advance, so no earth-science or astronomy expertise is needed.
  • Works where you live: the guide is written in US English, and every prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for spelling, units, and examples, including which season your hemisphere is having.
  • Safety built in: a never-look-at-the-sun rule for the sky weeks, and adult supervision for anything sharp, hot, outdoors, or after dark.

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 9–13. No earth-science or astronomy background needed, and no prior AI experience needed either. You lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and written out for you.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily answer "why do we have seasons?" and "what causes tides?" as two separate, disconnected facts, which is exactly how earth and space science turns into trivia nobody remembers or connects to what they see outside. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc where every new idea connects back to the one before it (a rock becomes soil, the ocean shapes the weather, the tilt of the Earth makes the seasons, the same sky holds the sun, moon, planets, and stars), Socratic prompts that make the tutor ask the learner to guess before it explains, guardrails that stop it from just lecturing, and checkpoints that prove the learner can read the world and the night sky for themselves. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need an earth-science or astronomy background? No. The AI tutor explains and asks the questions; you lead and keep sessions moving. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Works with any modern AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini), and the free version of each is enough; no paid plan needed. If you've never used an AI chat assistant before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes, so you'll be ready before Week 1.
  • Is my child's data collected? No. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise each session under your own account.
  • Does this replace school earth science class? It complements it. The goal here is understanding how the ground, water, weather, seasons, and sky connect as one system, which makes the vocabulary and facts from school actually make sense and stick.
  • Do we need a telescope or special equipment? No. Everything runs on things most families already have: a flashlight, an orange or ball, a notebook, and clear skies now and then.
