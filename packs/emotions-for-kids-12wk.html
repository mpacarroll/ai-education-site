Handle Big Feelings — A 12-Week Guide to Understanding Feelings and Staying Calm With an AI Tutor.

Short description (above the fold)

A done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding a 7–11-year-old from "I don't know what I'm feeling" to a personal calm-down plan they can actually use — naming feelings, noticing where they come from, breathing the body down, pausing before reacting, and solving the problem underneath — with an AI tutor that talks it through and role-plays the moment while you stay in the room. This is everyday emotional skill-building, not therapy, and you do not need to be an expert in feelings to lead it.

What you get

  • "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" — a five-minute onboarding in the front matter that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready, using a free AI assistant. No paid plan needed, and no prior AI experience required.
  • A parent/teacher orientation: how to sit beside a learner using AI, why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor, and exactly what your one ~60-minute session a week looks like.
  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each fully scripted: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan, copy/paste AI-tutor prompts at every step, a "show me" checkpoint — a small, real moment your child demonstrates for you — common mistakes to watch for, and practice. You lead; every step is timed and every prompt is written for you, so no expertise in feelings or in AI is needed.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when the learner is frustrated, bored, stuck, or checked out.
  • An AI-tutor prompt playbook — the Socratic starter prompt that turns the AI into a warm, age-appropriate feelings coach, and a guardrail that keeps the tutor from diagnosing or playing therapist.

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 7–11 who's ready to name big feelings, calm their body, and work through what's underneath instead of being run by it. No background in child psychology needed, and no prior AI experience needed — you lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and every AI prompt written for you, while the AI tutor does the talking-through and role-playing.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will either brush past a "I'm upset" or, worse, try to play therapist — neither teaches a lasting skill. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that builds from naming a feeling to a personal calm-down plan, a tutor that role-plays the moment (the mad friend, the big worry, the mistake) instead of just describing it, guardrails that keep the AI in "everyday skills coach" territory and firmly out of therapy territory, and checkpoints that always bring your child back to show you, live. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

Safety note

This guide teaches everyday emotional literacy — naming feelings, calming the body, working through a problem — and is not therapy, counseling, or mental-health treatment. The AI tutor is not a therapist and never diagnoses or labels a child. The adult leads every session with warmth and stays in the room the whole time. If a child shows lasting sadness, worry, or anger, or there is any safety concern, close the guide and talk to a qualified professional.
