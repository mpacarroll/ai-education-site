Who it's for

Adults who want to build simple furniture and fix things with wood and tools but have never made anything. No shop and no experience assumed; if you can clear a corner and hold a tape measure, you're ready.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily walk you into a cut with the guard off. This pack ships the structure and guardrails it lacks: a safety-first method, a real 12-week progression from first cut to finished build, and a tutor set up to confirm your protection is on and the blade is unplugged before you touch it, and to explain WHY instead of just handing you steps. Real competence with a safety net, not confident guessing.

Safety

This is educational content, not professional advice. Power tools and blades cause serious injury. It teaches confident-beginner-level building and repair and, just as importantly, how to work safely: eye, ear, and lung protection on, guards in place, beware kickback, never reach over a running blade, and unplug before changing bits or blades. Work within your comfort and stop when unsure. It is written for adults; a teenager using it needs an adult who knows these tools present for every blade and power-tool step.
