Safety

This is educational content, not professional advice. Power tools and blades cause serious injury. It teaches confident-beginner-level building and repair and, just as importantly, how to work safely: eye, ear, and lung protection on, guards in place, beware kickback, never reach over a running blade, and unplug before changing bits or blades. Work within your comfort and stop when unsure.
