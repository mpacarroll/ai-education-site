Who it's for

Job-seekers who want to present themselves clearly and interview with real confidence, whether you haven't interviewed in years, are switching fields, or just know your resume undersells you. No writing background assumed; you bring the raw material, the tutor helps you shape it.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will rewrite your resume if you ask, but it won't interview you like a hiring manager, catch a rambling answer, or push you to say a story again until it's tight. This pack ships the structure and rehearsal it lacks: a real 12-week progression from resume to story to composure, a tutor set up to ask before it tells, and a full mock-interview gauntlet with round-by-round feedback before it counts for real. Preparation with a coach, not a one-shot rewrite.

FAQ

  • Do I need any experience with resumes or interviewing? None. Week 1 starts with a six-second scan of what you already have; every skill is built in small, checked steps from there.
  • Which AI does it use? I've never used one. It works with any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini. The free version of any of them is enough. The guide opens with a "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" walkthrough that takes complete beginners from zero to ready in about five minutes, before Week 1 begins.
  • Does this cover salary negotiation? No, this pack is presentation and communication only: resumes, stories, and interview composure. It deliberately leaves out salary and financial talk.
  • Will it help with any industry? Yes. The method (scan-friendly resume, STAR stories, mock interview, composure) applies whether you're interviewing for your first role or your tenth.
  • Does it work outside the US? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for spelling, money, and examples, and to match local resume and interview conventions. For legal questions (what an employer may ask, work authorization), it points you to your country's official sources.
