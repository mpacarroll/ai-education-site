Kids in the Kitchen

Take the free placement check first: 15 minutes, and it names the week to start at.

Turn "can I help cook?" into real skill. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding an 8- to 12-year-old from washing their hands and mixing their first snack all the way to cooking a full meal for the family, using an AI tutor to coach technique while you supervise every step near heat or a blade. Safety first, always: you are there the whole time. You do not need to be a great cook to use this guide.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each built to run in about 60 minutes: a plain-language concept explainer, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, and practice to lock in the skill. Every step is scripted; you lead, and no cooking expertise is needed.
  • "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here": a five-minute front-matter walkthrough that gets a complete beginner from zero to a free AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini all work) ready to go, plus a parent/teacher orientation on why the adult stays the leader (and the supervisor) while the AI is the tutor.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when a session goes sideways (a learner who's frustrated or bored, a tutor that skips the adult step, or a check that the skill really landed), plus the safety rule that keeps the AI tutor from ever suggesting heat or blades without you present.
  • A digital guide, not a printable workbook: the value is in the worked examples, session plans, and checkpoints, not fill-in sheets.

  1. Welcome to the Kitchen: Safety & Clean Hands (the wash-up habit)
  2. No-Cook Kitchen: Snacks & Assembly
  3. Measuring & Mixing Like a Real Cook
  4. Safe Knife Skills (Adult Hands-On)
  5. Cracking Eggs & Building Breakfast
  6. First Stovetop Dish (Adult on Heat)
  7. A Salad or Side That's All Theirs
  8. Baking Something From Scratch
  9. Reading a Recipe Like a Chef
  10. Project Week I: Plan the Family Meal
  11. Project Week II: Cook the Family Meal
  12. Family Demo Day: Serve & Celebrate (and choose what's next)

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 8 to 12 who is ready to build real kitchen skills, not just "help out." No cooking expertise needed. You lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and written out for you, plus hands-on supervision at every step near heat or a blade.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily describe a recipe and leave a kid alone at the stove, which is exactly the wrong lesson. This pack ships the structure and safety a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that builds from no-cook assembly to a full family meal, Socratic prompts that make the tutor coach technique instead of just narrating steps, a guardrail that refuses to send a child near heat or a blade without an adult present, and checkpoints that prove the skill actually landed. Curriculum + safety guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need to be a great cook myself? No. The AI tutor coaches the technique; you lead by showing up and supervising. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Which AI does it use? Works with any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini. Free versions are enough; no paid plan needed, and no special software to buy.
  • I've never used an AI chat assistant. Can I still do this? Yes. The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes. Free versions of ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini are enough, no paid plan required.
  • Is my child's data collected? No. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise every AI session yourself, under your own account. Chats may be stored by the AI provider, so the guide has you share only a first name and age.
  • Who handles the knives, the stove, and the oven? You do, every time. This pack is built around adult supervision: the adult handles or closely guides all knives, the stove, the oven, hot oil, and boiling water, and the child never uses heat or a blade without direct, hands-on adult supervision. Mind food allergies and raw-food safety throughout, and use your own judgment. You assume all risk when following this guide.
  • What ages? Roughly 8 to 12.
  • Does it work outside the US? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every prompt tells the AI to use your country or region (as you give it, as it infers it, or by asking) for spelling, units, money, school terms, and examples.
