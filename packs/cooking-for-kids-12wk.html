Who it's for

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily describe a recipe and leave a kid alone at the stove — which is exactly the wrong lesson. This pack ships the structure and safety a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that builds from no-cook assembly to a full family meal, Socratic prompts that make the tutor coach technique instead of just narrating steps, a guardrail that refuses to send a child near heat or a blade without an adult present, and checkpoints that prove the skill actually landed. Curriculum + safety guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.
