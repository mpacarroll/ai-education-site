This 12-week plan teaches you the bike as five simple systems (rolling, stopping, going, steering, and the frame), so you can look at almost any symptom, work out where it lives, and know whether it's yours to fix. Who it's for

Adults who ride and want to maintain and fix their own bike: commuters, weekend riders, and parents keeping a household of bikes running. No mechanical experience assumed; if you can turn a wrench and read a symptom out loud, you're ready.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily talk you through a brake adjustment with no idea whether you actually got it right. This pack ships the structure and guardrails it lacks: a diagnose-by-system method, a real 12-week progression from your first flat to a full overhaul, and a tutor set up to make you double-check anything safety-critical, and to route you to a shop when a job is genuinely beyond confident DIY. Real mechanical understanding, not a memorized tip list.

Safety

This is educational content, not professional bike-shop advice. Brakes, steering, and frame integrity are safety-critical: the pack teaches you to recognize when a repair touching them is solid, and when to get it inspected by a shop before you ride. Work carefully around chains, spokes, and other moving parts, wear gloves with degreaser, and go slow when something feels off. On an e-bike, remove the battery before you work and leave the motor and electrics to a shop.

FAQ

  • Do I need any experience? None. Week 1 is literally learning to see the five systems on your own bike; every fix is broken into small, checked steps.
  • Which AI does it use? Works with any modern AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini), and the free version of any of them is enough. If you've never used one before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes before Week 1 starts.
  • Is this safe? Who's liable? It's educational only. Brakes, steering, and frame integrity are safety-critical. If a repair touches them and you're unsure, have a bike shop inspect it before you ride. You assume the usual DIY risk; work carefully around tools and moving parts.
  • What tools do I need? Basic hand tools: hex keys, tire levers, a spare tube, a floor pump with a gauge, a rag, lube, and degreaser by week 2. The guide says when later weeks add a spoke wrench, a multitool with a chain tool, and a quick link (plus a torque wrench for carbon parts).
  • Does it work outside the US? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every weekly launch prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for units (metric or imperial), spelling, and money.
