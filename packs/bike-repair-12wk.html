Who it's for

Adults who ride and want to maintain and fix their own bike — commuters, weekend riders, and parents keeping a household of bikes running. No mechanical experience assumed; if you can turn a wrench and read a symptom out loud, you're ready.

This pack ships the structure and guardrails it lacks: a diagnose-by-system method, a real 12-week progression from your first flat to a full overhaul, and a tutor set up to make you double-check anything safety-critical — and to route you to a shop when a job is genuinely beyond confident DIY. Safety

This is educational content, not professional bike-shop advice. Brakes, steering, and frame integrity are safety-critical: the pack teaches you to recognize when a repair touching them is solid, and when to get it inspected by a shop before you ride. Work carefully around chains, spokes, and other moving parts, and go slow when something feels off.
