Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 7–11. No science background needed, and no prior AI experience needed — each week is one fully scripted ~60-minute session where you lead, prompt, and supervise. The tutor does the explaining; you do the leading.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily explain the answer before your learner ever guesses — which skips the whole point. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that builds the scientific method one habit at a time (question, guess, fair test, observe, explain), guardrails that make the tutor ask before it tells, and checkpoints that prove the learner can do it themselves. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

Safety note

Every experiment uses simple household materials and is designed to be run with an adult supervising from start to finish. No open flames, no chemical mixing beyond the specific, safe combinations named in the guide, and no small parts or sharp tools near young children. Full safety details are in the pack's disclaimer and in the safety note attached to each week's activity.
