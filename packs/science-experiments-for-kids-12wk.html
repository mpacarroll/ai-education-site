Take the free placement check first: 15 minutes, and it names the week to start at.

Turn "why?" into a method. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding a 7–11-year-old from their first guess to designing and running their own safe experiment, using an AI tutor to do the questioning while you do the leading and the supervising. No science background required, and every experiment uses simple, safe household materials.

What you get

  • 12 deep, ready-to-run ~60-minute weekly sessions, each with a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, and practice. Fully scripted, with every step timed and written out for you, so no science or AI expertise is needed.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes. It works with any free AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini), no paid plan needed.
  • A parent/teacher orientation: how to sit beside a learner using AI, why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor, and exactly what your one ~60-minute session a week looks like, plus the safety rules that apply to every session.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when the learner is frustrated or bored, when the AI starts giving away answers, and for checking real understanding, plus a "don't give the answer" guardrail baked into every AI-tutor prompt.

The 12 weeks

  1. Meet the Scientist in You (what a scientist does: question and guess)
  2. Look Closely: The Power of Observing
  3. Your First Real Experiment (a safe kitchen experiment)
  4. Guess Before You Look: Predictions
  5. Fair Tests: Change Only One Thing
  6. Keeping Track Like a Scientist (recording what you see)
  7. Why Did That Happen? Explaining Results
  8. Ask Your Own Question
  9. Think Like a Detective: Putting It All Together
  10. Project Week I: Plan Your Own Experiment
  11. Project Week II: Run Your Own Experiment
  12. Family Demo Day: Show What You Found

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 7–11. No science background needed, and no prior AI experience needed. Each week is one fully scripted ~60-minute session where you lead, prompt, and supervise. The tutor does the questioning; you do the leading.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily explain the answer before your learner ever guesses, which skips the whole point. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that builds the scientific method one habit at a time (question, guess, fair test, observe, explain), guardrails that make the tutor ask before it tells, and checkpoints that prove the learner can do it themselves. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

Safety note

Every experiment uses simple household materials and is designed to be run with an adult supervising from start to finish. No open flames, no chemical mixing beyond the specific, safe combinations named in the guide, and no small parts or sharp tools near young children. Full safety details are in the pack's disclaimer and in the safety note attached to each week's activity.
