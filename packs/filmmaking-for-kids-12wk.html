Make Movies

Turn a phone in a drawer into a movie set. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding a 10–14-year-old from a shaky 10-second test shot to a real short film they premiere to the family (an actual story, real shots, and a simple edit), using an AI tutor that coaches like a director while you do the leading. You do not need any filmmaking experience.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each built to run in about 60 minutes and fully scripted: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint so you always know it landed, common mistakes to watch for, and practice to lock it in. No filmmaking expertise needed; every step is written for you.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes (no paid plan needed; any free AI assistant works), plus a parent/teacher orientation on why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor, and exactly what your one hour a week looks like.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for the moments every guide hits: the learner is frustrated or bored, the AI starts writing the script for them, or you want to check they really understand.
  • An AI-tutor playbook: the Socratic starter prompts that have the tutor coach from the learner's own idea and footage (never write the script or plan the shots for them), and a guardrail that keeps the tutor asking instead of directing the scene itself.

The 12 weeks

  1. A Movie Tells a Story: a first test shot with a real camera (the phone)
  2. The Idea and a Simple Script: a logline and a beginner shot list
  3. Shots and Angles: wide, medium, and close-up, on purpose
  4. Filming Steadily and With Light
  5. Sound Matters: clean dialogue without expensive gear
  6. Acting and Directing: giving notes, calling for another take
  7. Editing on a Phone: trim, reorder, cut on action
  8. Music and Titles
  9. A Full Scene, Start to Finish: the whole pipeline in miniature
  10. Project Week I: Plan It (script, shot list, actors, locations)
  11. Project Week II: Shoot and Edit It
  12. Premiere: Family Demo Day (present, reflect, choose what's next)

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 10–14 who wants to make something with a phone instead of just watching one. No filmmaking background needed: you lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, phone in hand, with every step timed and already written out for you.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily write the whole script or hand your learner a shot-for-shot plan, which teaches nothing about telling their own story. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc built on how movies actually get made (a story, shots and angles, steady footage and good light, clean sound, directing real takes, editing, music and titles), a Socratic playbook that has the tutor coach like a director reviewing dailies (asking what the learner was going for before offering a note), and checkpoints that prove each skill actually landed on real footage. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need any filmmaking experience? No. Every session is fully scripted: the concept, the worked example, the minute-by-minute plan, and the exact AI prompt are all written for you. You lead by showing up with a phone and encouragement.
  • Never used an AI chat assistant before? Which one does it use? Any modern AI assistant works (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini), and a free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan required. The guide's first section walks a complete beginner through setup in about five minutes, so you don't need to have used one before to start week 1.
  • Is my child's data collected? No. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise every AI session yourself, under your own account, including any time a clip is shared with the tutor.
  • Does it work outside the US? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every weekly prompt tells the AI to use your country or region (as you give it, as it infers it, or by asking you) for spelling, units, money, school terms, and examples.
  • What ages? Roughly 10–14. Age-banded variants are on the roadmap.
  • Refunds? Standard marketplace refund policy applies.
