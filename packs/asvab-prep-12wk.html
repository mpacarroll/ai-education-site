FAQ

  • Is this official ASVAB material? No. This pack is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Defense, any branch of the U.S. military, or the official ASVAB testing program, and it contains no official test content. Use it alongside the official ASVAB sample questions, your recruiter's guidance, and the current official format, which is the source of truth for content and rules.
  • Will this guarantee my score? No. No pack, book, or tutor can guarantee a score, a military qualification, or an enlistment outcome. This plan gives your effort real structure (diagnose, drill, time, review) and honest score tracking, not a promise.
  • Which AI does it use? I've never used one. Any modern AI assistant works (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini), and the free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan required. If you've never opened an AI chat before, the guide's first section walks a complete beginner through setup in about five minutes before Week 1 even starts.
  • Do I need to already be strong in all five sections? No. Week 1 is a full diagnostic that finds your real starting point, and the middle weeks drill exactly what's shaky or missing, including the Technical Subtests most people never study.
  • What if my score plateaus? The guide includes a plateau prompt: the tutor looks at your actual error pattern and changes the approach instead of just repeating the same drills.
  • Does this cover the CAT-ASVAB specifically? Week 2 explains how the computer-adaptive CAT-ASVAB behaves (including that you can't go back to review answers), and Week 12's dress rehearsal is designed to mirror those conditions as closely as possible.
