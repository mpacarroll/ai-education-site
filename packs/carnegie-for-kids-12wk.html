Make Friends and Speak Up

Inspired by classic "how to make friends" teaching, adapted for kids (an original curriculum, not affiliated with any commercial training organization). This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding an 8–12-year-old through the real, teachable skills of making friends and speaking up: being genuinely interested in other people, remembering names, starting a conversation, joining a group, disagreeing kindly, and standing up for themselves and others, with an AI tutor that role-plays every scenario so your child gets to practice safely before it counts for real. You do not need to be a social expert. You just need to sit beside them.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each fully scripted: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan, a guided AI-tutor role-play with the starter prompts built into every step (and what "good" looks like), common mistakes to watch for, practice, and a "show me" checkpoint (almost always a real moment with a real person), so you always know it landed.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes (any free AI assistant works: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini; no paid plan needed), plus a parent/teacher orientation on how to sit beside a learner using AI, why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor, and exactly what your one ~60-minute session a week looks like.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when the learner freezes or feels embarrassed, when the AI starts handing over the exact words, when the learner is bored, and to check the skill really transferred, plus a child-safety guardrail built into every weekly launch prompt.
  • Works wherever you live: the guide is written in US English, and every weekly session tells the AI to use your country or region (given, inferred, or asked) for spelling, money, school terms, and examples.
  • A digital guide you read on any device: worked examples, session plans, checkpoints, and rescue prompts are all there when you need them, session by session.

The 12 weeks

  1. Being Interested, Not Interesting: the #1 secret to being liked
  2. The Name Game: remembering names on purpose
  3. Starting a Conversation: openers that work anywhere
  4. Joining a Group: reading the room and finding a way in
  5. Being a Good Listener: making someone feel truly heard
  6. Being a Good Friend: the everyday habits that count
  7. Disagreeing Kindly: calm words instead of a fight
  8. Standing Up for Yourself and Others: a firm, kind voice
  9. Confidence and Handling Nerves: calming down before something brave
  10. Project Week I: Plan Your Challenge (a real, brave, friendly goal)
  11. Project Week II: Try It (the real challenge, for real)
  12. Demo Day: Confidence in Action (tell the story, celebrate growth, choose what's next)

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 8–12 who is ready to build real confidence with people: not a script to memorize, but a set of skills to practice. No social-coaching background needed, and you don't need to have used an AI assistant before. You lead one ~60-minute session each week, fully scripted, with every step timed and written out for you, while the AI tutor does the role-playing.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily tell your child exactly what to say, which builds a script, not confidence. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc built on the classic, teachable skills of getting along with people, a tutor that role-plays the hard moment (the new kid, the disagreement, the group already playing) instead of just describing it, guardrails that keep every scene warm and age-appropriate, and checkpoints that always send your child back out to practice with a real person. Curriculum + safe rehearsal + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Any modern AI assistant works (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini), and the free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. The guide's first section is a "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" walkthrough that gets a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes.
  • Is my child's data collected? No. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise every AI session yourself, under your own account. The guide shows you how to keep chats to a first name and age.
  • What ages? Roughly 8–12.
