Make Friends and Speak Up — A 12-Week Guide to Social Confidence and Kindness With an AI Tutor.

Short description (above the fold)

A "Dale Carnegie for kids." This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding an 8–12-year-old through the real, teachable skills of making friends and speaking up: being genuinely interested in other people, remembering names, starting a conversation, joining a group, disagreeing kindly, and standing up for themselves and others — with an AI tutor that role-plays every scenario so your child gets to practice safely before it counts for real. You do not need to be a social expert. You just need to sit beside them.

What you get

  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes — any free AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini) works, no paid plan needed — plus a parent/teacher orientation on how to sit beside a learner using AI, why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor, and exactly what your one ~60-minute session a week looks like.
  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each fully scripted: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan, a guided AI-tutor role-play with copy/paste starter prompts at every step (and what "good" looks like), common mistakes to watch for, practice, and a "show me" checkpoint — almost always a real moment with a real person — so you always know it landed.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when the learner is frustrated, bored, stuck, or going off track mid-session, plus the child-safety guardrail prompt built into every role-play.
  • A digital guide you read on any device — worked examples, session plans, checkpoints, and rescue prompts are all there when you need them, session by session.

The 12 weeks

  1. Being Interested, Not Interesting — the #1 secret to being liked
  2. The Name Game — remembering names on purpose
  3. Starting a Conversation — openers that work anywhere
  4. Joining a Group — reading the room and finding a way in
  5. Being a Good Listener — making someone feel truly heard
  6. Being a Good Friend — the everyday habits that count
  7. Disagreeing Kindly — calm words instead of a fight
  8. Standing Up for Yourself and Others — a firm, kind voice
  9. Confidence and Handling Nerves — calming down before something brave
  10. Project Week I: Plan Your Challenge (a real, brave, friendly goal)
  11. Project Week II: Try It — the real challenge, for real
  12. Demo Day — tell the story, celebrate growth, choose what's next

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 8–12 who is ready to build real confidence with people — not a script to memorize, a set of skills to practice. No social-coaching background needed, and you don't need to have used an AI assistant before — you lead one ~60-minute session each week, fully scripted, with every step timed and every AI prompt written for you, while the AI tutor does the role-playing.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily tell your child exactly what to say — which builds a script, not confidence. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc built on the classic, teachable skills of getting along with people, a tutor that role-plays the hard moment (the new kid, the disagreement, the group already playing) instead of just describing it, guardrails that keep every scene warm and age-appropriate, and checkpoints that always send your child back out to practice with a real person. Curriculum + safe rehearsal + a way to see progress. That's the difference.
