Biology shouldn't feel like a list of vocabulary words to memorize for a test. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding a 10–14-year-old through how living things actually work (cells, body systems, plants, and ecosystems) as one connected picture instead of disconnected facts, using an AI tutor to ask, explain, and adapt while you do the leading. You do not need a biology background, and you do not need to have ever used an AI chat assistant before; the guide gets you ready in about five minutes.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each with a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint so you always know it landed, common mistakes to watch for, and practice.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes, plus a parent/teacher orientation explaining how to sit beside a learner using AI and why the adult stays the leader while the AI is the tutor. You lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week: every step timed and written out for you, no expertise needed.
  • Four rescue prompts up front for when a session goes sideways: the learner is frustrated or bored, the AI explains too much before the learner has guessed, or you want to check they really understand.
  • Ask-before-it-tells tutoring in every session plan: each week's launch instructions have the AI ask before it tells (predict-first in every science week), and each session's checkpoint has tutor instructions tuned to it, all the way to a real investigation the learner designs, runs, and presents.

The 12 weeks

  1. Is It Alive? The characteristics of living things
  2. Cells: The Building Blocks of You
  3. Your Amazing Body Systems I: fuel and air (digestion, breathing, circulation)
  4. Your Amazing Body Systems II: command and support (brain, nerves, bones, muscles)
  5. Plants & How They Live: photosynthesis and plant parts
  6. Food Chains & Ecosystems: energy from the sun to the whole web
  7. Animals & Adaptation: why bodies and behaviors fit their environment
  8. Genes & Why You Resemble Family: a first look at heredity
  9. Classifying Life & Connecting the Dots: the whole pack as one big picture
  10. Project Week I: Plan Your Investigation (the learner's own testable question)
  11. Project Week II: Run Your Investigation
  12. Demo Day: Present Your Findings (celebrate, choose what's next)

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 10–14. No biology background needed, and no prior AI experience needed; your job is leading one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step written out for you.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily answer "what's a cell?" and "what's a food chain?" as two separate, disconnected facts, which is exactly how biology turns into a vocabulary list nobody remembers. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc where every new idea connects back to the one before it (a cell builds a body system, a body system keeps an organism alive, an organism has a role in its ecosystem), Socratic prompts that make the tutor ask the learner to predict before it explains, guardrails that stop it from just lecturing, and checkpoints that prove the learner can explain the connections themselves. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need a biology background? No. The AI tutor explains and asks the questions; you lead and keep sessions moving. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Works with any modern AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini); the free versions are enough, no paid plan required. The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes, so you can start from zero.
  • Is my child's data collected? No. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise each session under your own account.
  • Does this replace school biology class? It complements it. The goal here is understanding how living things connect as systems, which makes the vocabulary and facts from school actually make sense and stick.
  • We're not in the US. Does it still work? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every week's launch prompt tells the AI to use your country or region (as you give it, as it infers from how you write, or by asking you) for spelling, units, money, school terms, and examples.
  • What ages? Roughly 10–14. Age-banded variants are on the roadmap.
