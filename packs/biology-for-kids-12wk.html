Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 10–14. No biology background needed, and no prior AI experience needed — your job is leading one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every prompt written for you.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily answer "what's a cell?" and "what's a food chain?" as two separate, disconnected facts — which is exactly how biology turns into a vocabulary list nobody remembers. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc where every new idea connects back to the one before it (a cell builds a body system, a body system keeps an organism alive, an organism has a role in its ecosystem), Socratic prompts that make the tutor ask the learner to predict before it explains, guardrails that stop it from just lecturing, and checkpoints that prove the learner can explain the connections themselves. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.
