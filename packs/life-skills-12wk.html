Nobody sat you down and taught you how to shake a hand, set a table, tie a tie, or carry a conversation, and it's quietly stressful to feel behind on stuff everyone else seems to just know. This 12-week plan gives you the warm mentor you wish you'd had. The AI tutor doesn't lecture; it role-plays the real scenes (a dinner, an introduction, an awkward silence) and walks you through the home skills step by step, so you can practice, mess up, and get it right in total privacy. No shame, no experience assumed, just reps until it feels like yours.

What you get

  • A digital guide, deep enough to actually run from: 12 ready-to-lead ~60-minute weekly sessions.   • "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here": front-matter onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes, using a free AI assistant. No paid plan needed.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when a session goes sideways: when you feel behind or embarrassed, when the AI hands you a script instead of letting you practice, when it feels too easy, and when you want to check you really understand.
  • 12 weekly skills, each with a goal, a real-world worked example, a guided AI-tutor session (a role-play for the social skills, a step-by-step walk-through for the home skills, with the exact starter prompt written for you), and a "show me" checkpoint you demonstrate, not just read.
  • Session launch prompts that coach instead of lecture: each one has the AI let you try first, then coach you, role-playing the real scene in the social weeks, with a rescue prompt for when it starts handing you a script. They never make you feel stupid.
  • Plain safety notes where the home weeks call for them: iron and dryer safety, cleaning products that must never be mixed, and handling needles and pins.
  • A home routine and an event plan you build across the course and actually use.

You lead one ~60-minute session each week. Every step is timed and written out for you; no expertise needed, just follow the plan.

Who it's for

Young adults (and honestly anyone) who never got taught the practical social and household basics and feel a little behind. Judgment-free and no experience assumed. If you've ever quietly dreaded a dinner, an introduction, or an ironing board, this is for you.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will dump a wall of etiquette rules on you and call it a day. This pack ships the structure and safe practice it lacks: a real 12-week progression, and a tutor set up to role-play the actual awkward moments with you and walk you through the home skills hands-on (kindly, privately, and as many times as it takes), then coach one specific thing at a time. Rehearsed confidence, not a list you'll forget by Friday.

FAQ

  • Do I need any experience? None at all, and no one will know what you don't know. Week 1 is literally sitting down at a table; every skill is broken into small, rehearsable steps.
  • Never used an AI chat assistant before? That's fine. The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes.
  • Which AI does it use? Works with any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini. Free versions are enough; no paid plan needed, and no special software. Each service sets its own minimum age (Claude requires 18 or older), and the guide says so up front.
  • Isn't this stuff obvious? It isn't if nobody showed you, and most people were never shown. This pack assumes zero and never makes you feel behind for asking.
  • Is it private? No other person is watching. You practice with the AI, so you can fumble the handshake or the small talk a hundred times with zero audience before it counts for real. Chats may be stored by the AI provider, so the guide tells you what not to share.
  • Does it work outside the US? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every prompt tells the AI to use your country or region (as you give it, as it infers it, or by asking) for spelling, units, money, and examples, which matters for tipping, gifts, and dress codes.
