A masterclass you actually finish. Most people never learn the machinery underneath a good argument, so they lose debates they're right about, and get talked into things they shouldn't. This 12-week plan turns an AI tutor into a worthy opponent: it argues back, plants fallacies for you to catch, and steel-mans the other side so you're never winning against a weakling. You'll learn to build a case that holds (claim, reasons, evidence) and to take a bad one apart at the joint that actually fails. No philosophy background assumed, and no AI experience either, just a claim you care about and one ~60-minute session a week. This is a deep digital guide, not a quick worksheet: every week is fully scripted, so you're never guessing what to do or what to type.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions (~60 minutes each, fully scripted), every one with a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a timed session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, and practice.

  • The distinction smart people forget, taught in Week 2 and used every week after: whether an argument is well built and whether it's true are different questions, and confusing them is how you get fooled.
  • An AI-tutor playbook built into every session: a Socratic opponent launch prompt each week, a "level me up" prompt that sharpens the counter-argument, and a guardrail that won't let you win with a cheap trick.

Who it's for

Adults who want to reason clearly, win arguments honestly, and stop being fooled by slick rhetoric, useful for work, writing, debate, and everyday decisions. No philosophy background assumed. If you've ever known you were right but couldn't say why, this is for you.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot wants to agree with you, which is the opposite of what makes you a better thinker. This pack ships the structure and the opposition it lacks: a real 12-week progression from "what is an argument" to building and breaking one under fire, and a tutor set up to push back honestly, hide a fallacy in its reply for you to catch, and make the strongest case for the side you disagree with. You leave able to construct ideas that hold and dismantle ones that don't (generative and defensive), not just able to ask a bot what it thinks.

FAQ

  • Do I need any experience? None, and no philosophy background. Week 1 is just pulling one of your own opinions apart into a claim and a reason; every week builds one piece from there.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used an AI chat assistant before? It works with any modern AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini), and the free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. If you've never opened one, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes before Week 1 starts.
  • Isn't this just how to win arguments? It's how to win them honestly, and, just as much, how to lose gracefully when you're actually wrong. A whole week is about telling honest persuasion from manipulation, and the guardrail won't let you win with a cheap rhetorical trick.
  • Will it make me insufferable at dinner? The opposite, done right. Steel-manning and fair-first dismantling are the least insufferable habits there are: you learn to state the other side so well its holder agrees it's fair before you touch it.
