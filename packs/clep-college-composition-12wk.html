Pass CLEP College Composition — A 12-Week CLEP College Composition Prep Plan With an AI

Short description (above the fold)

Stop rereading the same grammar review and hoping it sticks. This 12-week plan starts with an honest diagnostic that maps your Multiple Choice skills and your two Essays into solid, shaky, and missing — then spends the middle weeks drilling exactly the gaps that map found, with an AI tutor that diagnoses before it teaches and never just hands you a finished thesis or paragraph. The last three weeks are full, timed practice with a review of every miss and every lost rubric point, ending in a dress rehearsal and an honest score estimate. No official test content, no score or credit guarantees — just a real plan, real rubric literacy, and a coach that keeps you honest.

What you get

This is a deep digital guide, not a shallow checklist: 12 ready-to-run, ~60-minute weekly sessions. You lead each session yourself — every step is scripted and timed, and every AI-tutor prompt is written for you to copy and paste, so no prior teaching or tutoring skill is needed. Each week includes: - A plain-language explanation of the week's concept and a concrete worked example. - A minute-by-minute session plan so you always know what to do next. - Copy/paste AI-tutor prompts at every step — diagnose, teach, quiz. - A "show me" checkpoint so you can confirm the skill actually landed. - Common mistakes to watch for, plus practice.

Also included: - An orientation that sets the weekly rhythm and the one rule that makes this work: review every miss and every lost rubric point until you can explain the "why," not just what the correct answer or stronger essay would have been. - A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" section that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes — no paid plan needed, any free modern AI assistant works. - Four rescue prompts in the front matter — a diagnose-first Socratic starter, a rescue prompt for when you're frustrated, a plateau-breaker for when a score or rubric row stalls, and a guardrail that bans answer-dumping (and essay-writing-for-you) and grade inflation. - A personal gap map you build in week 1 and revisit in week 12 to see exactly what moved.

Who it's for

Adults earning college credit by exam who want a real plan instead of a stack of random practice questions. It's self-directed — built to be run by the test-taker — and works whether you're starting from a strong base or feel behind on grammar, revision, or timed essay writing.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily write your thesis for you, which feels productive but teaches you nothing and won't be there on test day. This pack ships what a chatbot alone lacks: an up-front diagnosis that tells you whether Multiple Choice or the Essays need fixing first, a structured 12-week progression instead of random practice questions, real timed practice under the exam's actual section time limits (not untimed noodling), and a forced review of every miss and every lost rubric point for the underlying cause — a grammar gap, weak development, poor source use, or timing. A review book gives you the same practice set regardless of what you actually need; this pack adapts to your gap map.

What this pack offers is an honest diagnostic, focused practice on your actual gaps, and real timed review against real rubric language; the result depends on the work you put in and your school's own policy.
  • Which AI does it use? I've never used one.   • Do I need to already know my weak spots? No — week 1 is the diagnostic. You start wherever you actually are, not where you assume you are.
  • What if my essay score plateaus? The playbook includes a plateau prompt built for exactly this: it changes the approach and targets your specific error pattern instead of repeating the same drills.
