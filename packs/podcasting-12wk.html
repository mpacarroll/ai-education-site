FAQ

  • Do I need any experience? None. Week 1 is finding your show concept; every skill after that is broken into small, checked steps, and week 2 covers gear you likely already own.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini. Free versions are enough; no paid plan is required. If you've never used one before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes. No special software beyond that, though a way to record and share audio helps from week 2 on.
  • Do I need expensive equipment? No. The pack is built around good sound on a budget: your phone or a basic USB mic is enough to finish the season.
  • Will I actually publish something? Yes. Weeks 10 and 11 are a dedicated project to plan, record, and release one real episode to a live podcast feed.
  • Does it work outside the US? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every weekly launch prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for spelling, units, money, and examples, and the publishing week factors in hosting options where you live. For anything legal, like recording consent or music licensing, the guide points you to official local sources.
