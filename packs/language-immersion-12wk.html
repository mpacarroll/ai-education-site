Take the free placement check first: 15 minutes, and it names the week to start at.

Children learn languages by needing them, not by conjugating. This 12-week plan makes real immersion practical at home: the AI tutor speaks, listens (voice mode), corrects gently, and never tires of repetition. Rules of the road: speak out loud from day one, comprehension before correctness, grammar as patterns you notice rather than rules you memorize. Pick your language in week 1; the plan works for any of them.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly plans, each about 60 minutes of talking split into six 10-minute spoken sessions: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a session-by-session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, and optional practice to extend the habit.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes, with free AI assistants and no paid plan needed.
  • Four rescue prompts up front: for a discouraged learner, a tutor that starts lecturing, a learner who's coasting, and checking they really understand rather than just echoing.
  • You run the short daily sessions, fully scripted, with every step timed and written out for you. No expertise, and no prior AI experience, needed.
  • Safety built in: you run every session under your own account, mission calls are only with people you know, and an adult handles the stove and knives for a cooking mission.

Who it's for

Parents guiding a learner aged 8+, or any adult beginner. Language-agnostic: choose Spanish, French, Mandarin, or whatever you like in week 1. You don't need to speak it; the tutor does, and you keep the weekly momentum. The guide is written in US English, and every weekly launch prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for spelling, units, money, and examples.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

Many language apps lean on tapping and reading; a raw chatbot has no plan. This pack gives you immersion with a spine: speaking out loud from day one, a real 12-week arc toward a mission the learner completes in the language, and gentle-correction prompts that keep it encouraging. Structure plus voice practice a flashcard app can't touch.

FAQ

  • Do I need to speak the language? No. The tutor is the fluent one; you lead the habit and ask for the weekly "show me."
  • Which language? Any language your AI assistant speaks well. You pick in week 1 and the whole plan adapts. Widely spoken languages get the strongest support, especially in voice mode.
  • Which AI tutor does it use? Any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini. Free versions are enough; no paid plan required. Use the app's voice mode if you can. Never used one before? The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes.
  • Is my child's data collected? Not by us. It's a digital guide sold to you, the adult, with no login and no child account. Sessions run under your own AI account, and the AI provider may store those chats, so the guide has you share only first names and ages and stay present for every session.
