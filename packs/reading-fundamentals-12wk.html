The 12 weeks

  1. Meeting the Alphabet — mapping which letter sounds are solid, shaky, or new
  2. Blending Sounds into Words — sliding sounds together, faster each round
  3. Word Detective: Sight Words — the first ten instant words
  4. First Sentences — reading short sentences made of known words
  5. Cracking New Words — a repeatable sound-it-out strategy
  6. Little Stories Together — a tiny decodable story, read together
  7. Growing the Word Bank — more sight words, plus sh, ch, th
  8. Reading With Expression — matching your voice to the punctuation
  9. Building the Daily Habit — a ten-minute routine and a "just right" book
  10. Project Week I: Pick Your Book (a real reading plan)
  11. Project Week II: Read It — working through the book to the end
  12. Story Time: Demo Day — read the whole book aloud, choose what's next

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 5–8 who is just starting to read or still building fluency. Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily read the word out loud the moment a child hesitates — which teaches nothing. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc from letter sounds to a finished book, Socratic prompts that make the tutor wait for the child to try first, a guardrail that keeps it from ever handing over the word, and checkpoints that prove the skill actually landed. Curriculum + guardrails + a bottomless supply of fresh, just-right practice. That's the difference.
