Safety

This is educational content, not professional advice. Sharp knives and sharpening tools cut deeply — always cut and sharpen with the edge moving away from yourself, keep your fingertips curled under in "the claw," work slowly and deliberately on the stone, and never test an edge by running your finger across it. Keep blades out of reach of children.
