Who it's for

Home cooks who want genuinely sharp knives and safe, fast, confident cutting. Zero experience assumed. If you own a kitchen knife and a cutting board, you're ready to start; you'll add a honing rod by week 4 and a sharpening stone by week 5.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will hand you a generic sharpening-angle number and move on. This pack ships the structure and guardrails it lacks: a real mental model of how an edge cuts (angle, apex, burr) so you become generative (able to sharpen and maintain any knife, not just follow a tip list), a 12-week progression, and a tutor set up to check your claw grip and slow you down on the stone instead of rushing you toward a "good enough" edge. Understanding, not gadgets.

Safety

This is educational content, not professional advice. Sharp knives and sharpening tools cut deeply: always cut and sharpen with the edge moving away from yourself, keep your fingertips curled under in "the claw," work slowly and deliberately on the stone, and never test an edge by running your finger across it. Keep blades out of reach of children, and if a teenager uses the course, an adult should supervise every session with a blade or stone.
