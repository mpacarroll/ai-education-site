Tell a Great Story

Ages 8-12 · Reading and writing

Take the free placement check first: 15 minutes, and it names the week to start at.

Turn screen time into something that teaches. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding an 8–12-year-old from noticing what makes a story fun to inventing and telling one of their own to the family, using an AI tutor that acts as an audience and a story coach, never a ghostwriter. Your child learns to build a hero who wants something, a problem that's in the way, and a turning point where things change, so the story is always theirs. You do not need to be a writer.

What you get

  • 12 weekly sessions, each a ready-to-run ~60-minute plan: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint (almost always your child telling or performing something out loud), common mistakes to watch for, and practice. Fully scripted: every step timed and written out for you. No writing background or AI expertise needed.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes (any free AI assistant works; no paid plan needed), plus a short parent/teacher orientation on why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor (and audience), never a ghostwriter.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when the learner is stuck or bored, the AI starts writing the story for them, or you want to check they really understand.

The 12 weeks

  1. What Makes a Story Grab You? Noticing the hook in a favorite story
  2. A Hero Who Wants Something
  3. The Problem and the Stakes
  4. Beginning, Middle, End
  5. Showing, Not Telling
  6. A Big Turning Point
  7. Voices and Description
  8. A Satisfying Ending
  9. Put It All Together: the whole story, start to finish
  10. Project Week I: Plan Your Story (the learner's first outline)
  11. Project Week II: Tell It
  12. Demo Day: perform for the family, reflect, choose what's next

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 8–12 who loves stories and wants to start making their own. No writing background needed, and you don't need to have used an AI assistant before. Your job is leading one ~60-minute session each week, fully scripted, and being a great audience.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily write your child a whole story on request, which teaches nothing and leaves the imagination out of it entirely. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that builds the actual craft of storytelling (a want, a problem, a turning point, a satisfying ending), a guardrail that keeps the tutor acting as an audience and coach instead of an author, Socratic prompts that make it ask before it tells, and checkpoints that prove the story is coming from your child. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need to be a writer myself? No. The AI tutor coaches and listens; you lead by showing up and asking for the checkpoint. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Any modern AI assistant works (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini). A free version is enough; no paid plan needed. The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes, so you're ready even if you've never opened an AI chat before.
  • Is my child's data collected? Not by us. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise every AI session yourself, under your own account, and the guide tells you what to keep out of the chat.
  • We're not in the US. Does it still work? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every launch prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for spelling, money, school terms, and examples.
  • What ages? Roughly 8–12.
