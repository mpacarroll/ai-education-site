Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 8–12 who loves stories and wants to start making their own. No writing background needed, and you don't need to have used an AI assistant before — you lead one ~60-minute session each week, fully scripted, and being a great audience.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily write your child a whole story on request — which teaches nothing and leaves the imagination out of it entirely. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that builds the actual craft of storytelling (a want, a problem, a turning point, a satisfying ending), a guardrail that keeps the tutor acting as an audience and coach instead of an author, Socratic prompts that make it ask before it tells, and checkpoints that prove the story is coming from your child. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.
