Turn "that's not fair" into a real argument. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding an 11–14-year-old through the real, teachable skills of reasoned thinking: a claim needs a reason, evidence beats opinion, a strong case takes the other side seriously, and disagreeing respectfully is a sign of strength, not weakness, with an AI tutor that plays a friendly opponent and points out sloppy reasoning, so your child learns to build a real case instead of just arguing louder. You do not need to have debated anything in your life.

What you get

  • 12 ready-to-run ~60-minute weekly sessions. Each one is a deep, scripted guide: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in (where the tutor argues back a little), a "show me" checkpoint so you always know it landed, common mistakes to watch for, and optional practice for the week.
  • A parent/teacher orientation, including a "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" walkthrough that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes (no paid plan needed; any free AI assistant works), plus why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when the learner is stuck or shut down, the AI is overpowering them instead of coaching, practice feels boring, or you want to check they really understand.
  • A digital guide: worked examples, session plans, checkpoints, and the tutor prompts that run them, no printing required.

The 12 weeks

  1. What Makes an Argument: a claim needs a reason
  2. Fact, Opinion, and Evidence
  3. Building a Case: claim, reasons, evidence
  4. Walking in Their Shoes: steel-manning the other side
  5. Spotting Sloppy Reasoning: catching bad arguments
  6. Asking Sharp Questions
  7. Staying Calm and Respectful
  8. Changing Your Mind Is Strength
  9. A Friendly Debate With the Tutor
  10. Project Week I: Plan Your Debate (a real family topic)
  11. Project Week II: Debate It (practice against the tutor)
  12. Family Demo Day: the real debate, then choose what's next

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 11–14 who argues plenty already and is ready to get good at it: building a reasoned case, backing it with evidence, and disagreeing without a fight. No debate background needed and no prior AI experience needed. You lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and already written out for you, while the AI tutor does the sparring.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily agree with whatever your child argues, or argue so hard it shuts them down. Either way, nothing gets built. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that moves from "a claim needs a reason" to a real debate in front of the family, a tutor that plays a genuine but friendly opponent and flags sloppy reasoning instead of just agreeing, guardrails that keep every round respectful, and checkpoints that prove the skill transfers to real conversations, not just chats with an AI. Curriculum + a worthy opponent + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Will this just teach my kid to argue with me more? It teaches them to argue better: with a reason, with evidence, and calmly. The guardrail keeps every round about the idea, never the person, and the checkpoints reward staying respectful as much as being right.
  • Do I need to know anything about debate myself? No. The AI tutor plays the opponent and does the sparring; you lead by showing up and asking for the checkpoint. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Which AI does it use? I've never used one. That's fine; this pack is built for you. It works with any modern AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini), and the free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. The guide's first section walks a complete beginner through setup in about five minutes before Week 1 starts.
  • Is my child's data collected? Not by this guide. It's a digital guide sold to you, the adult, with no login and no child account. You start and supervise every AI session yourself, under your own account. The AI provider may store those chats, so the guide shows you what to keep out of them (a first name and age are all it needs).
  • We're not in the US. Does it still work? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every AI prompt tells the tutor to use your country or region (the one you give, one it infers from how you write, or one it asks you for) for spelling, units, money, school terms, and examples.
  • What ages? Roughly 11–14.
