Safety note

Every hands-on activity in this guide uses simple household materials and is designed to be run by, or directly supervised by, an adult from start to finish. Cleaning chemicals are never mixed together, flames and heat sources stay under adult control at all times, and no step goes beyond what a given week explicitly names as safe. Full safety details are in the pack's disclaimer and in the safety note attached to each week's activity.
