Intro to Chemistry

Ages 11-15 · Science

Turn "what's it made of?" into real understanding. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding an 11–15-year-old through the real ideas of chemistry (atoms, molecules, states of matter, reactions, acids and bases), using an AI tutor to do the explaining while you do the leading and the supervising. Every hands-on moment uses safe, adult-run household demonstrations, so your learner sees the science, not just hears about it. You do not need a chemistry background.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each fully scripted end to end: a plain-language concept explanation, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in (nothing to write yourself), a "show me" checkpoint so you always know it landed, common mistakes to watch for, and independent practice.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes. A free AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini all work) is all you need, no paid plan required.
  • A parent/teacher orientation: how to sit beside a learner using AI, why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor, and exactly what to expect from your one ~60-minute session each week, plus the safety rules that apply to every session.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter (ready to use the moment the learner is stuck or frustrated, bored, or just repeating words back, or the AI starts explaining instead of asking), plus a predict-first, "don't give the answer" guardrail baked into every session.
  • You lead one ~60-minute session a week. It's fully scripted: every step is timed and written out for you, including what to say to the AI tutor. No chemistry background and no prior AI experience needed.

The 12 weeks

  1. What Is Chemistry, Really? (surfacing what your learner already thinks)
  2. Everything Is Made of Atoms
  3. Molecules and Why Water Is Special
  4. States of Matter and Changes
  5. Mixtures vs. Pure Substances
  6. A Safe Reaction: signs of chemical change (adult-run)
  7. Acids and Bases: a homemade indicator, safely tested
  8. The Periodic Table, Gently
  9. Energy in Reactions: heat released or absorbed
  10. Project Week I: Plan a Safe Investigation
  11. Project Week II: Run the Investigation
  12. Demo Day: Show the Science (present it, choose what's next)

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 11–15. No chemistry background needed, and no prior AI experience needed; your job is one fully scripted ~60-minute session a week, plus curiosity and hands-on supervision. The tutor does the explaining; you do the leading and the supervising.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily define "atom" and move on, which teaches vocabulary, not understanding, and it has no idea what's actually safe to try at your kitchen table. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that builds real chemistry ideas in order, Socratic prompts that make the tutor ask the learner to predict before it explains, guardrails that keep every hands-on step inside safe, adult-supervised limits, and checkpoints that prove the learner understands it, not just watched it. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

Safety note

Every hands-on activity in this guide uses simple household materials and is designed to be run by, or directly supervised by, an adult from start to finish. Cleaning chemicals are never mixed together, flames and heat sources stay under adult control at all times, and no step goes beyond what a given week explicitly names as safe. Full safety details are in the guide's safety box and in the instructions for each hands-on week.

FAQ

  • Do I need a chemistry background? No. The AI tutor explains and questions; you lead and supervise. Every session comes with the exact prompts to paste, in order.
  • Never used an AI chat assistant before? That's fine. The guide opens with a "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" walkthrough that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes.
  • Which AI does it use? Works with any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini. The free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan is required. The prompts work in any of them as-is, no special software to buy.
  • Is my child's data collected? No. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult. There's no login and no child account; you start and supervise every AI session under your own account. The AI service you choose has its own privacy policy, and the guide lists what not to paste into a chat.
  • Who supervises the hands-on parts? You do, directly, every time. This guide uses only safe household materials, never asks you to mix cleaning chemicals, and keeps any flame or heat source under adult control. It's educational content, not professional advice, and you assume the risk of any hands-on activity you choose to do.
  • What ages? Roughly 11–15.
  • Does it work outside the US? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every launch prompt tells the AI to use your country or region (as you give it, as it infers it, or by asking) for spelling, units, money, school terms, and examples.
