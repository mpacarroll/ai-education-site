A masterclass you actually finish, on the one thing overhead every single day. This is not a weather app and not a list of folk sayings. It's 12 weeks of understanding the atmosphere as a system (pressure, air masses, fronts, moisture and dew point, clouds, wind, and terrain) so you can look up, read what you see, and forecast the next few hours yourself. Each week an AI tutor explains the mechanism in plain language, then questions you as you reason your own observations into a forecast, and has you predict-then-score against what actually happens. No science background needed. It's a teacher, not an official forecaster, and it says so, every time the weather turns dangerous.

Format: A digital guide with 12 ready-to-run weekly sessions. You lead one ~60-minute session each week, and it's fully scripted: every step timed and written out for you, no expertise needed. Each week ends with a "show me" checkpoint, so you know the read has clicked before you move on.

Who it's for

Hikers, gardeners, parents, sailors, and the plain curious who want to understand and predict the weather by observation instead of just refreshing an app. If you've ever looked up and wondered "is that rain coming or not?", this teaches you to answer it. No science background assumed.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will hand you a generic weather explanation and a false sense of certainty, and it can't see your sky. This pack ships the structure and guardrails it lacks: a real 12-week progression through the atmosphere's system, a predict-then-score method that actually builds skill, and a tutor set up to reason a forecast WITH you from your own observations, while deferring to official warnings and telling you to shelter the moment the weather turns dangerous. Understanding you can use, not answers on tap.

Safety

This is educational content only: amateur observation, not a substitute for official forecasts, watches, or warnings. Always check your national weather service and heed local authorities. Never take risks in severe weather: seek shelter from lightning, never enter or drive through floodwater, and get off ridgelines and open water when storms threaten. Reading the sky sharpens your judgment; it does not replace the professionals whose job is to keep you safe.

  • Is this a weather app or a forecasting tool? No, and that's the point. It teaches how the atmosphere works so you can read the sky yourself and judge any forecast, instead of blindly trusting one more app.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Works with any modern AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini), and a free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. If you've never used an AI chat assistant before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes, so you're ready before Week 1 starts. No special software or sensors required (a phone barometer app is a nice-to-have, not a must).
  • I'm not in the US. Does it still work? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every prompt tells the AI tutor to use your country or region for spelling, units (Celsius or Fahrenheit, hPa or inHg), money, and examples. The guide points you to your own national weather service for official warnings.
  • Is this a substitute for official forecasts and warnings? Who's liable? No. It's educational only and it's amateur observation. Official forecasts, watches, and warnings always win, and you should heed local authorities. The tutor is a teacher, not a meteorologist, and cannot see your sky. You assume all risk, and you never take chances in severe weather.
  • Will it keep me safe in a storm? No, that's the job of your national weather service and local authorities. This pack teaches you to recognize dangerous signs early so you shelter sooner, not to ride storms out.
