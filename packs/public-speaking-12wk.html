Who it's for

Adults who dread presenting (or who are competent but want to be genuinely compelling) at work, in meetings, on stage, or on video. Nerves and inexperience are assumed. If you have a voice and something you'll one day need to say to a room, you're ready.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily write your speech for you, which teaches you nothing and leaves you memorizing someone else's words. This pack ships the structure and coaching it lacks: a real 12-week progression, a coach set up to make you speak and fix one thing at a time, filler-word counting in voice mode (checked against your own recordings), and a role-played hostile Q&A that builds real composure. Your skill, your words, not a script you'll blank on.

FAQ

  • Do I need any experience? None; nerves are assumed. Week 1 is literally calming the fear and saying one sentence out loud; every skill builds from there in small reps.
  • I've never used an AI chat assistant. Is that a problem? No. The guide opens with a "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" section that walks a complete beginner through setup in about five minutes, before Week 1 even begins.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI chat assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, and similar all work, and the free version of any of them is enough. A voice mode works best, and most of these apps offer one (features vary by app and change often); typing works in a pinch. The prompts work anywhere, with no special software or paid plan.
  • Do I have to speak out loud? Yes, that's the whole point, and it's why it works. You practice privately with the AI before you ever face a real room.
  • I only need this for one big talk. Will it help fast? Yes. Weeks 10 and 11 build and rehearse one real talk end to end, so you can jump there, but the earlier weeks are what make it land.
