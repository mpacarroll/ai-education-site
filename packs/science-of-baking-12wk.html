Safety

This is educational content, not professional advice. A working baking kitchen means hot ovens and surfaces that burn, stand mixers and blades that injure, and raw flour and raw eggs that can make you sick before they're baked. Use oven mitts, keep hands clear of running mixers and sharp blades, never taste raw flour or raw egg, and know your household's allergies — gluten (wheat), nuts, dairy, and eggs are all common and serious. Stop when unsure; you assume all risk.
