FAQ

  • Do I need a good memory or any experience? No. The whole premise is that memory is trained, not inherited. Week 1 sets your baseline; every week beats it with technique, not effort.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini. Free versions are enough; no paid plan needed. Never used one before? The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes, so you're ready before Week 1 starts.
  • Is this just mnemonics tricks? The tricks are real (palaces, major system, absurd images), but the point is a system: encode deliberately, then use spaced testing so it lasts. The tutor enforces the testing part most people skip.
  • How is this different from a spaced-repetition app? Apps schedule reviews of cards you still have to encode somehow. This teaches the encoding techniques and the spacing, and the tutor tests your actual recall instead of trusting you tapped "I knew it."
