Who it's for

Adults who know how the pieces move but want to actually get good at chess through understanding, not by memorizing openings or copying engine lines. If you've ever lost a game and had no idea why, this pack is for you.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily hand you "the best move" and let you nod along, learning nothing. This pack ships the structure it lacks: a real 12-week progression through the principles that actually govern good chess, a tutor set up to demand your reasoning before it gives you its own, and checkpoints that make sure you can apply each idea to a position you've never seen, not just recite it back.   • Do I need any experience? Just knowing how the pieces move. Week 1 starts by assessing where you actually are, not where a syllabus assumes you are.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, or similar. A free version of any of these is enough; no paid plan required. Never used an AI chat assistant before? The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes. No special software and no chess engine required.   • Will this teach me openings to memorize? No, on purpose. It teaches the principles that generate good opening moves in any position, which is what actually improves your chess, unlike a memorized repertoire that falls apart the moment your opponent deviates.
