FAQ

  • Is this official? No. This pack is not affiliated with or endorsed by the test maker, and it contains no official test content. Use it alongside official College Board materials and current exam rules; formats and dates change, so verify them yourself.
  • Will it guarantee my score or AP credit? No. No honest prep pack can guarantee a score or college credit. This plan maximizes your preparation; the work (and the review of every miss) is yours.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Works with any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini. Free versions are enough; no paid plan required. Each service sets its own minimum age (Claude requires 18 or older), so students under 18 should set up an account with a parent or guardian. If you've never used an AI chat assistant before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes before Week 1 starts.
  • Do I need a calculator or equation sheet? For the May 2027 exam, a four-function, scientific, or graphing calculator on the College Board's approved list is allowed (Bluebook also has a built-in Desmos calculator), and the equation sheet is provided. Practice with a printed copy of the official sheet, and check the current rules before test day.
  • What if I'm strong in some topics already? Week 1's diagnostic maps solid/shaky/missing so you can spend Weeks 2-8 on what actually needs the time, not everything equally.
  • I don't live in the US. Will it work for me? Yes. The guide is written in US English, and every AI prompt tells the tutor to use your country or region for spelling, school terms, and examples. The physics stays in SI units, just like the real exam.
