FAQ

  • Do I need any experience? None. Week 1 assumes nothing beyond curiosity; every idea starts from an object you can see or touch, not a formula.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini. Free versions are enough; no paid plan required. If you've never used one before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes.
  • Is it safe? The hands-on parts use ordinary household things (a spoon, a straw, a rubber band, a mug). The electricity week stays conceptual, with a battery and LED or a flashlight only, never household wiring, and the steps that need one (hot water, a candle, a rolling chair, a stretched rubber band) carry their own short safety note.
  • Is there math? Almost none by design; the goal is the mental model, not the equation. If you want the math later, the tutor will take you there when you ask.
  • How much time per week? You lead one ~60-minute session, fully scripted, every step timed and written out for you, no expertise needed. The two project weeks run at the same pace.
