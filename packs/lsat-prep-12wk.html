Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will answer whatever question you paste in and move on, with no diagnosis, no timing discipline, no record of what you actually got wrong last week. This pack ships the structure a free chatbot doesn't have: an adaptive Week 1 diagnosis that targets the weeks that follow, real timed practice under test-day conditions starting in Week 9, and a guardrail that forces a review of every miss for the why instead of just moving on to the next question. FAQ

  • Is this official LSAT material? No. This pack is not affiliated with or endorsed by the LSAT's test maker and contains no official test content. Use it alongside the official test-maker's practice materials and current LSAT rules; formats and timing change, so verify them yourself.
  • Will this guarantee a score or admission? No, and treat anyone who promises that with suspicion. This pack guarantees no score and no admission outcome. It guarantees a real, honest, diagnose-and-drill process. What you make of it is on you, and that's the truth of any prep plan.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI chat assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini. Free versions are enough; no paid plan is required. Never used one before? The guide's opening section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes, and every session after that is written out step by step.
  • Do I need to already be good at logic or reading? No. Week 1 is a diagnostic specifically so the tutor can meet you where you are, whether that's "never seen an LR question" or "stuck at a score for months."
  • What if my score plateaus? That's what the plateau prompt in the playbook is for. It's built into the plan, not a sign you're doing it wrong.
