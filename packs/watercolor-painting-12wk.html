A masterclass you actually finish, and the one that finally explains why watercolor keeps "doing its own thing." It isn't bad luck or bad brushes: watercolor is a conversation between pigment, water, and paper, and almost every blotch, bloom, or muddy patch traces back to a water ratio or timing you didn't yet know to control. This 12-week masterclass teaches the medium's actual behavior (water control, washes, mixing, layering, value) in order, so you learn to paint intentionally instead of reacting to whatever the water already did. Photograph your wash or painting and the AI tutor diagnoses the water-pigment-paper relationship behind it, then gives you one specific adjustment to try. No natural talent and no expensive supplies required.

Format: a digital guide with 12 ready-to-run, fully scripted ~60-minute sessions, one a week. You lead each session yourself; every step is timed and written out for you in advance, so no art background or AI experience is needed. A basic student-grade set, a few brushes, watercolor paper, tape, a palette or white plate, and water are all you need.

Who it's for

Adults who want to paint with watercolor and have never picked up a brush, plus anyone who has tried before and ended up with muddy, blotchy, unpredictable results. No experience assumed; if you can hold a brush and point a phone camera, you're ready.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

Ask a raw chatbot "why did my wash bloom" and you'll get generic advice that could apply to anything. This pack ships the method a chatbot lacks on its own: a real progression through watercolor's actual behavior (water load, paper wetness, timing, layering) and a tutor set up to diagnose your specific photo against that behavior instead of guessing. You leave able to predict what the water will do before you touch the brush to paper, not able to follow one more tutorial.

Format & honesty note

This is a masterclass in structure and depth, taught the way you'd want a real watercolor class taught, but it's an honest, self-paced pack, not a live course or a certification. The teaching comes from you doing the painting each week and an AI tutor coaching your real washes and paintings from photos. No hype, no "unlock your inner artist," just the trainable mechanics of the medium, taught in order.

FAQ

  • Do I need any experience? None. Week 1 starts with watching a drop of water dry on paper, and every technique builds from there in small, checked steps. Each ~60-minute session is fully scripted, timed, and comes with the exact AI prompts to use, so you're never guessing what to do next.
  • What supplies do I need? A basic student-grade watercolor set, a couple of brushes, watercolor paper, masking or painter's tape, a palette or plain white plate, two water jars, and a paper towel. Nothing fancy; this is about controlling water, not owning gear.
  • Never used an AI chat assistant before? That's fine; the guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI assistant with photo input: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, and similar all work, and the free version of any of them is enough. You show it your wash or painting; the prompts work anywhere, no special software or paid plan required.
  • Will it just tell me my painting looks great? No. The tutor is set up to diagnose the actual water-pigment-paper relationship behind what happened and give you one specific adjustment, not empty praise.
  • Is this messy or expensive to start? Watercolor is one of the lower-cost, lower-mess painting mediums: a basic starter set is inexpensive, and cleanup is just water.
