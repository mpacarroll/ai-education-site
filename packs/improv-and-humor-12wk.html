Who it's for

Adults who want to be quicker on their feet, funnier, and more at ease socially: in meetings, at parties, on dates, at the dinner table. Zero stage experience assumed; you never have to set foot on a stage to get everything out of this pack.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily write you jokes to repeat, which teaches you nothing and leaves you dead in the water the moment the room doesn't go as scripted. This pack ships the structure and live practice it lacks: a real 12-week progression through the actual principles of improv, a tutor set up to run scenes with you and make YOU find the funny, and a project that takes it out of the practice room and into one real moment in your actual life. Quick on your feet, not quoting someone else's punchline.

FAQ

  • Do I need any experience? None. Zero stage time assumed. Week 1 starts with short yes-and scenes; every skill builds from there in small, live reps.
  • Which AI does it use? Works with any modern AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini), and the free version of any of them is enough, no paid plan needed. Never used one before? The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes. The tutor plays scenes opposite you and coaches from there; the prompts work anywhere, no special software.
  • Do I have to perform on a stage? No. Every scene happens privately with the AI tutor; the final project is one real moment you choose (a toast, a meeting, a dinner), not an open mic.
  • I just want to be funnier in conversation, not do "improv." That's exactly what this teaches: improv is the training method, everyday quickness and humor are the point. The principles (yes-and, listening, status, finding the game) are the same reflexes that make you sharp at a dinner table.
