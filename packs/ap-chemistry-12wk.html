Short description (above the fold)

Stop rereading the same unit summary and hoping the calculations click on test day. This 12-week plan starts with an honest diagnostic that maps your Multiple Choice and Free Response skills — atomic structure, bonding, stoichiometry, thermodynamics, kinetics, equilibrium, acids and bases — into solid, shaky, and missing, then spends the middle weeks drilling exactly the gaps that map found, with an AI tutor that diagnoses before it teaches and never just hands you a finished calculation. Who it's for

Students taking AP Chemistry who want a real plan instead of a stack of random practice problems. It's self-directed — built to be run by the student — and works whether you're starting from a strong base or feel behind on bonding, equilibrium, or timed free-response writing.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily hand you the final number for a calculation, which feels productive but teaches you nothing and won't be there on test day. This pack ships what a chatbot alone lacks: an up-front diagnosis that tells you which units to fix first, a structured 12-week progression instead of random problem sets, real timed practice under section time limits with the actual calculator policy (not untimed noodling), and a forced review of every miss and every lost point for the underlying cause — content gap, calculation error, missing units, or timing. A review book gives you the same problem set regardless of what you actually need; this pack adapts to your gap map.
