Who it's for

Adults who can only follow a recipe step-by-step and want to actually cook, by taste and instinct, not by instruction. Zero real technique assumed. If you own a knife, a pan, and a willingness to taste as you go, you're ready.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will dump a recipe on you and call it teaching. This pack ships the structure and guardrails it lacks: a taste-first method, a real 12-week progression, and a tutor set up to ask what you see, smell, and taste before it tells you anything, and to stop you at raw-meat, hot-oil, and allergy lines. You learn to read the food, not just read the page.

Safety

This is educational content, not professional advice. Cooking means sharp knives, high heat, open flame, hot oil, and raw meat. Keep a working smoke alarm and a fire extinguisher within reach, keep raw meat and its juices away from ready-to-eat food, cook proteins through, watch hot oil, know your household's food allergies, and stop the moment you're unsure. If a child or teen cooks with you, an adult stays in the kitchen.
