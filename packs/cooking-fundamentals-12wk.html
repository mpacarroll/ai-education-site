Safety

This is educational content, not professional advice. Cooking means sharp knives, high heat, open flame, hot oil, and raw meat. Keep a working smoke alarm and a fire extinguisher within reach, keep raw meat and its juices away from ready-to-eat food, cook proteins through, watch hot oil, know your household's food allergies, and stop the moment you're unsure.
