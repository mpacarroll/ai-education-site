A masterclass you actually finish. Anyone can learn a song by rote; this teaches you the grammar of music so you can write your own and explain why any piece feels the way it does. Over 12 weeks an AI tutor walks you through the mechanism behind the feeling: why a chord sounds sad, why a progression pulls toward home, why one note changes everything. It sets up examples for you to play and name by ear and (the move that makes it stick) has you predict how a change will feel before you hear it. By the end you're generating music, not copying it.

Format: a digital guide with one ~60-minute session each week, fully scripted: every step timed and written out for you, so no music or AI expertise is needed going in. Instrument-agnostic: bring a keyboard, a guitar, or a free piano app.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each built the same way: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute ~60-minute session plan with the AI-tutor instructions built in, an ear-training move, a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, and practice.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes. Any free AI assistant works (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini), no paid plan needed, plus the one rule of the whole season: never accept a fact you can't hear.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when your ear feels stuck, the tutor starts stating facts instead of making you hear them, it all feels too easy, or you want to check you truly understand an idea rather than just repeat the term.
  • An original short piece (8 to 16 bars) you compose and can explain, choice by choice, by the final weeks.

Who it's for

Adults who love music and want to understand it from the inside, whether you can already play a little or have never touched an instrument. If you've ever wondered why a song gives you chills and wished you could make one that does the same, this is for you. No sheet-reading required.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will dump theory jargon on you (scales, modes, the circle of fifths) and leave you none the wiser about what any of it sounds like. This pack ships the structure it lacks: a first-principles progression from a single interval to a finished piece, and a tutor set up to make you hear and predict before it ever explains, so understanding lands in your ear. You leave able to generate and explain your own music, not reciting terms you can't use.

FAQ

  • Do I need to read music or play an instrument? No. You need one thing that makes pitch: a keyboard, a guitar, or a free piano app on your phone, and week 1 gets you oriented on it.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Works with any modern AI chat assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini. The free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. If you've never used one before, the guide's first section walks you from zero to ready in about five minutes. Every session is written out step by step, so there's nothing to figure out on your own. A tutor that can describe or play audio examples helps the ear-training but isn't required; you'll do most of it on your own instrument.
  • Is this really a "masterclass"? It's a paid pack framed like one: a real 12-week course you work through yourself, honestly priced as a one-time purchase, not a subscription or a live class.
  • I think I'm tone-deaf. Can I still do this? Almost certainly yes. The tutor starts you on single "higher or lower / brighter or darker" distinctions and builds your ear from there.
