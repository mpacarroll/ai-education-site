Your First Piano — A 12-Week Guide to Playing Piano From Scratch With an AI Tutor.

Short description (above the fold)

You sit down at a keyboard for the first time, and by week 12 you play a real piece for someone. This 12-week plan turns an AI tutor into a patient teacher on the bench next to you: you describe what you played (or record a short clip and tell it what you hear), and it asks questions until you both understand what's working — then walks you through the fix in small, checked reps. No prior experience, no "having an ear" required. You learn the keyboard map, both hands, how to actually read music, and how to play something real — not just drills you'll forget.

What you get

  • A digital guide with a "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" walkthrough that takes a complete beginner from zero to ready in about five minutes — no paid plan needed, any free modern AI assistant works.
  • Four rescue prompts up front for when a session goes sideways, so you're never stuck waiting on a fix.
  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each fully scripted for one ~60-minute sit-down at the keyboard: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan, copy/paste AI-tutor prompts at every step, a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, and practice to lock it in. You lead the session — every step is timed and every prompt is written for you, so no piano or teaching expertise is needed.
  • A finished piece you can actually play for someone by the end of the season, plus your next practice goal.

Who it's for

Adult beginners sitting down at a keyboard for the first time — no lessons as a kid, no reading music, no idea where middle C is, and no prior experience with AI chat tools. If you have a keyboard or piano and can set aside one ~60-minute session a week, you're ready — every session is scripted for you, start to finish.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily teach you a song by rote and skip the parts that actually make you a musician. This pack ships the structure it lacks: hands-separate-before-together as a hard rule, real notation reading instead of memorized finger patterns, and a 12-week arc that ends with you understanding what you're playing — not just repeating it. You leave able to read a new piece, not just perform the one you memorized.

FAQ

  • Do I need any experience? None. Week 1 is literally finding middle C and learning to sit at the instrument; every skill is broken into small, checked steps.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI chat assistant — ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini — and the free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan required. Voice or video input helps for a few weeks but isn't required, describing what you played works fine.
  • Never used an AI chat assistant before? That's fine — the guide opens with a "Start here" section that walks a complete beginner through setup in about five minutes, before Week 1 even starts.
  • Do I need to read music already? No — that's taught from scratch in weeks 6 and 7, built on the keyboard skills from weeks 1-5.
  • What if I don't own a piano? A basic 61-key keyboard is enough for the whole pack.
