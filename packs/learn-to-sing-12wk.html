FAQ

  • Do I need any experience or the ability to read music? No. Week 1 starts with a recording of you singing as you already sing; every skill after that builds in small, checked reps.
  • Which AI does it use? Any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, and similar. A live voice mode helps most. Voice modes differ (some hear only your words, not your pitch), so the guide pairs the AI with a free tuner app and your own recordings. Free versions are enough; no paid plan required. Never used one before? The guide's first section walks complete beginners through setup in about five minutes, then the tutor coaches you from what it hears, your tuner readings, and your recordings.
  • Do I have to actually sing out loud? Yes. That's the whole point, and it's why it works. Reading about breath support doesn't train your breath; singing does.
  • I just want to nail one song for an event. Will this help fast? Yes. Weeks 9 to 11 take you from choosing a song to a rehearsed, full-tempo performance of it, but the earlier weeks are what make it actually land instead of sounding forced.
  • Is it safe for my voice? The guide trains gentle, supported singing, tells you to stop at pain or strain, and says when to see a doctor or speech-language pathologist. It isn't medical advice.
