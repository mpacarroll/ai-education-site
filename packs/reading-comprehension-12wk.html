Read to Understand

Sounding out the words isn't the same as understanding the story. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding an 8–12-year-old from decoding to real comprehension (main idea, inference, questioning the text, summarizing in their own words), using an AI tutor to ask great questions about any book or article they bring, while you do the leading. You do not need to be a reading teacher.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly sessions, each built to run in about 60 minutes and fully scripted: a plain-language concept, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in, a "show me" checkpoint, common mistakes to watch for, and independent practice.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes about five minutes: gets a complete beginner from zero to ready using a free AI assistant, no paid plan needed.
  • A parent/teacher orientation: how to sit beside a learner using AI, why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor, and exactly what your one weekly session looks like.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when the learner is frustrated or bored, when the AI starts handing over answers, or when you want to check they really understand.
  • A digital guide: no printing required, no expertise needed. Every step is timed and written out for you.

The 12 weeks

  1. Meet Your Reading Tutor: reading is thinking, not just decoding
  2. Reading Like a Detective: Ask As You Go (questions before, during, and after)
  3. What Is This Really About? Main idea vs. topic
  4. The Details That Matter
  5. Reading Between the Lines: inference
  6. Say It In Your Own Words: summarizing, not copying
  7. Characters & Why They Act
  8. Fact or Opinion? Spotting persuasion
  9. When the Words Get Hard: a toolkit for tough texts
  10. Project Week I: Choose Your Deep Read (a real book or article)
  11. Project Week II: Go Deep
  12. Demo Day: Show What You Understand (and pick the next goal)

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged 8–12. No reading-teacher background needed, and no AI experience needed; you lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and written out for you. Works whether your learner is a reluctant reader, an eager one who skims too fast, or anywhere in between. The guide is written in US English, and every weekly launch prompt tells the AI to use your country or region for spelling, units, money, school terms, and examples.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily summarize the book for your learner, which teaches nothing. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc from decoding to deep comprehension, Socratic prompts that make the tutor ask questions instead of handing over the main idea, guardrails that stop it from summarizing on request, and checkpoints that prove the learner can do the thinking themselves. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Do I need to be a reading teacher? No. The AI tutor asks the questions; you lead. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one? Works with any modern AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini. Free versions are enough; no paid plan needed. If you've never used an AI chat assistant before, the guide's first section walks you through setup in about five minutes, no prior experience required.
  • Is my child's data collected? Not by us. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult: no login and no child account. You start and supervise each session under your own AI account, and the guide shows you what not to share, since chats may be stored by the AI provider.
  • What if my child hates the book we picked for the project weeks? The plan step in Week 10 is designed to catch that early: the tutor helps you pick something genuinely interesting before the deep-read weeks begin, and swapping texts mid-project is fine.
  • What ages? Roughly 8–12. Every weekly launch prompt includes your learner's age, so the tutor pitches passages and questions to their level.
