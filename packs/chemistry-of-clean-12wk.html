Safety

This is educational content, not professional advice. Never mix cleaning products: bleach with ammonia, or bleach with acids like vinegar or rust removers, releases toxic gas that can seriously injure or kill, and bleach is only ever diluted with plain water. Always ventilate, wear gloves and eye protection, and test any cleaner on a small hidden spot first. Keep all products away from children and pets, and store them in their original, labeled containers. Read manufacturer labels and follow them. Stop the moment you're unsure.
