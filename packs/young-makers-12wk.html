Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 10–14 who likes making things — with their hands, with words, with whatever's in the junk drawer. No inventing, engineering, or design background needed, and no prior AI experience needed — you lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week, with every step timed and every AI prompt written for you.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily hand your kid a finished idea or a fully designed solution the moment they ask — which skips the entire skill this pack teaches. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that runs the actual maker loop (notice, imagine, build, test, improve), Socratic prompts that make the tutor ask before it tells, a guardrail that stops it from ever designing or building the invention for the learner, and checkpoints that prove the child can do it themselves. Curriculum + guardrails + a way to see progress. That's the difference.
