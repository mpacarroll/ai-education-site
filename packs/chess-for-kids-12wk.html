Turn screen time into a chessboard. This is a done-for-you, 12-week plan for guiding a 7–12-year-old from learning how the pieces move to playing (and enjoying) a real game with the family, using an AI tutor that teaches chess from the ideas up: how the pieces work together as a team, how to keep your king safe, and how to think one move ahead, instead of a list of openings to memorize. The tutor plays real games with your child and reviews them afterward. You do not need to know how to play chess.

What you get

  • 12 deep weekly session guides, each with a plain-language concept explanation, a concrete worked example, a minute-by-minute session plan with the AI-tutor prompts built in (the exact prompts to paste and what "good" looks like) where the tutor teaches, plays, and reviews real chess with your child, a "show me" checkpoint so you always know it landed, common mistakes to watch for, and practice.
  • A "Never used an AI chat assistant? Start here" onboarding that takes about five minutes and gets a complete beginner from zero to ready. No paid plan needed; any free AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini) works.
  • A parent/teacher orientation covering how to sit beside a learner using AI, why the adult stays the leader and the AI is the tutor, and exactly what leading a session looks like: you lead one ~60-minute session each week, fully scripted, with every step timed and written out for you. No chess background or AI expertise needed.
  • Four rescue prompts in the front matter for when the learner is stuck or frustrated, is bored, or the AI starts handing over moves, plus one to check real understanding, plus the "never just hand over the winning move" guardrail baked into every session.

The 12 weeks

  1. Meet the Board: files, ranks, and squares
  2. How the Pieces Move
  3. Piece Teamwork & Value: point values and protection
  4. Protecting Your King: check and castling
  5. Controlling the Middle: opening principles
  6. Simple Tactics: The Fork
  7. Simple Tactics: The Pin
  8. Thinking One Move Ahead
  9. A Simple Checkmate
  10. Project Week I: Plan a Game (a real game plan)
  11. Project Week II: Play and Review (a full game against the tutor)
  12. Family Demo Day: the real game, then choose what's next

Who it's for

Parents, teachers, and homeschoolers guiding a learner aged roughly 7–12 who's curious about chess or already owns a set that's gathering dust. No chess background needed, and no AI experience needed. You lead one fully scripted ~60-minute session each week (every step timed and written out for you), and the AI tutor does the teaching, the playing, and the reviewing.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will either crush your child at chess or just hand over the winning move. Either way, nothing gets learned. This pack ships the structure a chatbot lacks: a real 12-week arc that builds from "how does a knight move" to a real game played in front of the family, a tutor that teaches the ideas behind good moves instead of just making them, a guardrail that stops it from handing over answers, and checkpoints that prove the skill transfers to a real board, not just a chat window. Curriculum + a patient opponent + a way to see progress. That's the difference.

FAQ

  • Will the AI just beat my child every game? No. The guardrail keeps the tutor playing at a level where your child can win sometimes, and it always explains its reasoning afterward, win or lose. The goal is understanding, not a perfect record.
  • Do I need to know how to play chess myself? No. The AI tutor teaches the rules, plays the games, and reviews them; you lead by showing up and asking for the checkpoint. Every session comes with the exact prompt to start it.
  • Do we need a real chess set? It helps, but any free chess app or website works fine; the pack is written to work with either.
  • Which AI does it use? What if I've never used one before? Works with any modern AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini), and the free version of any of them is enough; no paid plan needed. If you've never used an AI chat assistant, the guide's first section walks a complete beginner through setup in about five minutes before Week 1 starts.
  • Is my child's data collected? Not by us. This is a digital guide sold to you, the adult, with no login and no child account. You start and supervise every AI session yourself, under your own account. The AI provider may store those chats, so the guide has you share only a first name and age.
  • What ages? Roughly 7–12.
