Who it's for

Professionals in any field who want to genuinely be good at using AI for real work, not just dabbling. No technical background assumed, and no prior AI experience required. The front-matter onboarding gets complete beginners set up with a free AI assistant in about five minutes. If you have work you'd like done faster, you're ready.

Why this instead of a free chatbot

A raw chatbot will happily hand you a confident, wrong answer and let you ship it. This pack ships the structure and judgment it lacks: a real 12-week progression, coaching on your own tasks, and a tutor set up to make you verify facts, numbers, names, and quotes before you trust them, and to tell you plainly when a task involves data you shouldn't paste or a decision AI shouldn't make. Skill with a safety net, not confident guessing.
