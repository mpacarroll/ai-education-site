Homeschool Algebra Curriculum: How to Choose One

What to look for in a homeschool algebra curriculum for ages 12 to 16, how to place your child first, and where a 12-week AI tutor plan fits.

2026-11-26

Search for a homeschool algebra curriculum and you get two kinds of answer: long lists of publishers, and forum threads where parents describe what worked for one child. Both are useful. Neither tells you what your child needs, because the right algebra course depends on where your child is right now, not on their grade.

This page is for a parent choosing algebra for a child of about 12 to 16. It covers what to look for, how to check readiness first, and where our own guide fits and where it does not.

Who this is for

  • Parents of a child who is about to start algebra, or who started and stalled.
  • Parents who do not remember their own algebra class and worry about teaching it.
  • Families who want a plan that runs in one guided hour a week.

Start with placement, not a publisher

Algebra sits on top of earlier skills: negative numbers, fractions, and the idea that a letter stands for a number you have not found yet. A child who is shaky on those will struggle in any algebra course, however good it is. So the first step is a short check.

Run the free algebra placement check. It is a 15-minute chat with any AI assistant. It asks one question at a time, gives no hints, and ends with a report that names the week to start at. If the check shows gaps below algebra, start with the free math placement check instead, which reaches back to arithmetic and fractions.

What to look for in any algebra curriculum

It explains why, not only how. A course that teaches "move the number to the other side" without the balance idea behind it leaves a child stuck the first time a problem looks new.

It asks your child to explain. Getting the right answer is not the same as understanding it. Look for a course that asks "how do you know?" on a regular basis.

It has a clear arc. Expressions, then equations, then graphing, then word problems is a common and sensible order. Be wary of a course that jumps around.

It fits your time. A daily textbook lesson and a weekly guided session are different commitments. Pick the one your family will keep doing in week 8.

You can run it. If you need to re-learn algebra before you can teach it, the course will stall when you are busy.

Where our guide fits

Algebra Made Clear is a 12-week plan for ages 12 to 16. It is a PDF you run with any AI chat assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot, and the free versions work. Each week is one scripted session of about 60 minutes. You paste the prompts, the AI tutor asks before it tells, and every step is written out for you.

The weeks move from what a letter means, through the balance scale, one-step and two-step equations, negative numbers and like terms, to graphing a line and turning word problems into equations. The last three weeks are a project your child plans, solves and explains.

The tutor rules in every prompt tell the AI not to hand over answers, even if your child asks. That matters more in algebra than almost anywhere, because a chatbot will happily solve the equation and teach nothing.

What it does not do

  • It is not a full Algebra 1 textbook course with hundreds of graded problems. It is a concept-first plan that gets a stuck child unstuck and ready for one.
  • It does not grade or keep records for you.
  • It does not replace your judgment. You are in the room, and you decide when to move on.

Questions parents ask

What is the best curriculum for homeschooling algebra?

The one that starts where your child actually is and makes them explain their steps. A full textbook course suits a child who is ready for Algebra 1 and works well alone. A shorter, concept-first plan suits a child who is stuck on what the letters mean. Place your child first, then choose.

What is the hardest homeschool curriculum?

There is no single answer, because hard depends on the fit. A course that starts above your child's real level feels hard no matter how it is written. That is why a short placement check before buying saves more frustration than any choice of publisher.

How many hours a day should a 7th grader homeschool?

It varies by family and by state rules, so check what applies where you live. For algebra alone, steady short practice beats long sessions. Algebra Made Clear is built on one guided hour a week, plus optional practice on other days.

What are the top 5 homeschool curriculum?

Rankings online mix very different things: full textbook series, video courses and short guides. Compare them on three questions instead: does it place your child first, does it make them explain their thinking, and can you run it without remembering your own algebra class.
