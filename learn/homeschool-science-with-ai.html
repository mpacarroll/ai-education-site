Homeschool Science With an AI Tutor: Experiments, Biology and Chemistry

How to run hands-on homeschool science with a free AI chat assistant: real experiments, fair tests, and a project of your child's own, from age 7 to 15.

2026-11-02

Science is the subject where an AI tutor helps most and has to be handled most carefully. It helps because it can ask the question a good science teacher asks: "what do you predict, and why?" It needs care because science is something you do with your hands, not something you read about on a screen.

The rule: the AI asks, your child does

In every science session: - The AI asks for a prediction first. Before any experiment, your child says what they think will happen. - Your child runs the experiment with real materials, and you're there. - The AI asks why. After the result, it asks your child to explain what happened, rather than explaining it for them.

Where to start, by age

  • About ages 7 to 11: Little Scientist. It starts with Meet the Scientist in You, then observing closely, a first real experiment, predictions, and fair tests (change only one thing). It ends with your child planning and running their own experiment for a family demo day.
  • About ages 10 to 14: Life Science. It runs from Is It Alive? through cells, body systems, plants, food chains, adaptation and genes, to an investigation of their own.
  • About ages 11 to 15: Intro to Chemistry. It covers atoms, molecules, states of matter, mixtures, a safe reaction, acids and bases, and the periodic table, gently, then a safe investigation they plan themselves.
  • About ages 11 to 15: Physics: How Things Move. Forces and motion, with the same ask-then-do approach.

Questions parents ask

Can AI replace hands-on science?

No, and it shouldn't. In our courses the AI asks questions and helps plan; your child does the experiment with real materials at the kitchen table. The AI never replaces doing it.

Are the experiments safe?

They use household materials and an adult is present for every session. Chemistry weeks use safe reactions and say so explicitly. Always read the week's safety notes before starting.

What age should we start homeschool science?

Little Scientist starts at about age 7 with observing and simple experiments. Life Science, Intro to Chemistry and Physics follow for about ages 10 to 15.
