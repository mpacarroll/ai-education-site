AI Homeschool Curriculum: What to Look For

What an AI homeschool curriculum should do and what to avoid: structure, tutor rules that stop answer-giving, placement, and the parent's role.

2026-11-26

An AI homeschool curriculum can mean very different things. Some are full apps with a built-in AI teacher and a monthly subscription. Some are a list of prompts. Some are structured courses that use an AI chat assistant as the tutor. The label does not tell you much. What matters is whether the course has a real plan, whether the AI teaches or just answers, and whether you stay in charge.

This page is about curriculum structure: what an AI-led course should include. If your question is which AI tool to use, see which free AI works for homeschool. If you want to set up ChatGPT as a tutor today, see how to use ChatGPT as a homeschool tutor.

Who this is for

  • Parents curious about AI for homeschool but unsure what a course should look like.
  • Parents who tried an AI chat assistant and found it just did the homework.
  • Families who want structure without a new app or subscription.

What a real AI homeschool curriculum includes

A sequence. Twelve weeks that build in order teach more than a different prompt every day. Ask what week 8 covers and why it comes after week 7.

Placement before teaching. The course should find where your child is first, then start there. A free placement check is how every guide here begins.

Rules for the AI, in writing. An AI chat assistant left to itself will answer the question. A curriculum has to tell it, every session, to ask before it tells and not to hand over work your child could copy. See AI tutor prompts that don't give answers for what those rules look like.

Checks that the learning stuck. Look for moments where your child explains or shows what they learned, not just finishes a worksheet.

A clear role for you. You should know what to do in each session, even if you do not know the subject.

Honest limits. A course should say what it does not do. Be wary of promises about grades or test scores.

Questions to ask before you buy any AI course

  • Does it work with the AI you already have, or does it need its own app?
  • Is there a monthly fee, and what happens to your child's work if you stop paying?
  • Is your child talking to the AI alone, and is that suitable for their age? The AI safety setup checklist covers age minimums and setup.
  • Can you see every prompt the AI is given?

Questions parents ask

What is an AI homeschool curriculum?

It is a course where an AI does part of the teaching: explaining, asking questions and giving feedback. Some are apps with their own AI built in. Others, like the guides here, are written plans you run in an AI chat assistant you already have.

Is there a free AI homeschool curriculum?

The AI side can be free: the free versions of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot all run these guides. The placement checks on this site are free too. The 12-week guides themselves are paid downloads from our Etsy shop.

Can AI replace a homeschool teacher?

No, and a good AI curriculum should not try. The AI can explain and question patiently. You decide what your child learns, notice when they are tired or stuck, and keep the AI to its rules.

How do I stop the AI from just giving my child the answers?

Give it rules at the start of every session, in writing, and stay nearby. Every weekly prompt in these guides starts with tutor rules that tell the AI to ask before it tells and never to give an answer your child could copy. The guide to tutor prompts that do not give answers shows how.
