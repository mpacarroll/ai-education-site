Homeschool Earth Science Curriculum: One System

Choosing a homeschool earth science curriculum: rocks, water, weather and space for ages 9 to 13, and AP Environmental Science for teens.

2026-11-26

Most homeschool earth science curriculum is a textbook with a chapter per topic: rocks, then oceans, then weather, then space. Each chapter makes sense on its own, and children rarely see that they are one story. The part that sticks is the connection: water shapes rock, the sun drives the weather, and the tilt of the earth makes the seasons.

This page is for parents of children about 9 to 13, and of teens heading toward environmental science, choosing an earth science plan. It covers what to look for and where our two guides fit.

Who this is for

  • Parents of a child who asks why it rains, or why winter is cold.
  • Families who want hands-on observation without buying a kit.
  • Parents of a teen who wants an environmental science exam course at home.

What to look for in a homeschool earth science curriculum

One connected system. Ground, water, air and sky should link, week to week.

Observation. A daily log of weather and sky teaches more than a diagram. Your child should collect real data.

Common myths named. Many adults think seasons come from the earth getting closer to the sun. A good course surfaces that belief and tests it.

Models at home. A flashlight and a ball can show day, night, seasons and the moon's phases.

An investigation. A small project, planned and run by your child, ends the course on their own question.

Where our guides fit

Earth and Space Science is a 12-week plan for ages 9 to 13. It is a PDF you run with any AI chat assistant, and the free versions of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot all work. Each week is one scripted session of about 60 minutes that you lead. The weeks cover earth's layers, a daily observation log, rocks and soil, water and oceans, weather and the water cycle, the seasons, the sun, moon and tides, the planets, and stars and our galaxy. The last three weeks are an earth or space investigation your child plans, runs and presents.

Ace AP Environmental Science is a 12-week study plan for a teen taking that exam. It starts with a check of where they stand, works through ecosystems, populations, earth systems, land and water use, energy and pollution, then drills the exam's math and ends with timed sections and a full dress rehearsal. It is meant to be used with your official course materials.

In both, the AI tutor asks for a guess before it explains.

Questions parents ask

Is there a secular homeschool earth science curriculum?

Earth and Space Science is not written from a religious point of view. It teaches the ground, water, weather, seasons and sky as one connected system, with observations your child makes at home.

What about environmental science for homeschool?

For a teen ready for an exam course, Ace AP Environmental Science is a 12-week study plan built to be run by the student. It covers ecosystems, populations, land and water use, energy and pollution, plus the math the exam asks for.

Is there a free homeschool earth science curriculum?

The night sky, a weather log and a library field guide are free and teach a lot. A plan adds order, so each week builds on the last. Our earth and space placement check is free and names the week to start at.

Do we need a telescope or special equipment?

No. The younger plan uses things most families have: a flashlight, an orange or ball, a notebook and a clear night now and then.
