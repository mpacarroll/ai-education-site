Is AI Safe for Kids? A Homeschool Parent's Setup Checklist

A practical checklist for using AI chat assistants with children: who holds the account, what to paste first, and what to watch for.

2026-11-02

The honest answer to "is AI safe for kids" is that it depends far more on the setup than on the brand of assistant. Here is the setup we use in every one of our guides.

1. The adult holds the account

Each AI service sets its own minimum age, and some require users to be 18. Rather than work around that, keep it simple: the account is yours, and the session runs on your device or with you nearby. Your child does the thinking and typing. You own the account and the room.

2. Turn off what you don't need

Most assistants let you turn off chat history or memory, and some let you opt out of having conversations used for training. Check the settings page of the one you use. For a learner, fewer features is usually better.

3. Paste tutor rules before anything else

An AI assistant follows instructions at the top of a conversation. A short rules block changes its job from answering to tutoring: ask before telling, one small hint at a time, never write the learner's work, and stay on today's topic. That last rule matters for safety as much as for learning. If the conversation drifts to something unrelated, the tutor brings it back.

4. Give it a narrow goal

"Help with math" invites a wandering conversation. "Today's goal: add fractions with unlike denominators, and end with three problems I do on my own" keeps the session short and focused.

5. Teach your child that AI can be wrong

AI assistants make mistakes, sometimes confidently. Turn that into a lesson: when the AI says something surprising, ask "how could we check that?" A learner who checks the machine is learning the most useful skill of the next decade.

6. Never share personal details

First name and age are plenty. No surnames, addresses, school names or photos of the child. Our prompts only ever ask for a first name and an age.

7. Look at the end of every session

Thirty seconds of scrolling back through the conversation tells you how it went. You will see whether the tutor held its rules and whether your child did the work.

Questions parents ask

What age can a child use ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?

Each service sets its own minimum age in its terms, and some require users to be 18. Read the terms for the assistant you choose. The simplest setup for younger learners is that the adult holds the account and runs the session, with the child at the keyboard beside them.

Can an AI tutor give a child wrong information?

Yes. AI assistants make mistakes, sometimes confidently. That is one reason a good tutor prompt asks the learner to check their own work, and one reason an adult stays in the room.

Should my child use AI alone?

Not at first. Sit in on the first sessions. Once you have seen the tutor hold its rules and your child use it well, you can step back, but a quick look at the session at the end stays a good habit.
