Homeschool High School Chemistry: Two Paths

Homeschool high school chemistry: an intro course for ages 11 to 15, an AP Chemistry study plan for teens, what labs need, and a free placement check.

2026-11-26

Homeschool high school chemistry is where many families get stuck. It needs math, labs and an adult who remembers the subject, and few parents have all three. Two things help: making sure the foundation is solid before the hard course starts, and, for a teen headed to the exam, a study plan that finds the gaps instead of rereading everything.

This page is for parents of teens about 13 to 17 planning chemistry, and of younger students about 11 to 15 building the base. It covers what to look for and where our two guides fit. Our intro guide is written for ages 11 to 15, so for most high schoolers it is the foundation year, not the full credit.

Who this is for

  • Parents of a teen about to start chemistry who want the basics solid first.
  • Families of a teen taking AP Chemistry who want structured exam prep.
  • Parents without a chemistry background.

What to look for in high school chemistry at home

A solid base. Atoms, molecules and reactions should make sense before stoichiometry does.

Predict, then see. The learner should guess what will happen before a demonstration, then explain the gap.

Safety owned by an adult. Any hands-on step should be adult-run and inside clear limits.

Units and justification. In AP Chemistry, a right number without units or a reason loses points.

Timed practice. For the exam, full timed sections with every miss reviewed matter more than more reading.

Where our guides fit

Intro to Chemistry is a 12-week plan for ages 11 to 15. It is a PDF you run with any AI chat assistant, and the free versions of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot all work. Each week is one scripted session of about 60 minutes. The weeks cover what chemistry is, atoms, molecules and water, states of matter, mixtures and pure substances, a safe reaction, acids and bases with a homemade indicator, the periodic table, and energy in reactions, then a safe investigation your learner plans, runs and presents. Every hands-on step is a safe, adult-run household demonstration.

Ace AP Chemistry is a 12-week study plan for a teen taking that exam. It starts with a check of where they stand, then covers the exam and calculator rules, atomic structure, bonding, stoichiometry, thermodynamics, kinetics, and equilibrium with acids and bases, then three timed practice weeks and a full dress rehearsal.

In both, the AI tutor does not hand over a finished calculation.

A common path is the intro plan in the year before high school chemistry, then a full course with a textbook and lab, then the AP study plan in the spring of the exam year. The placement checks tell you whether your teen can skip ahead.

What they do not do

  • They are not a full lab course. Neither ships chemicals or lab gear.
  • The intro plan is not a full high school credit on its own.
  • The AP plan does not replace your course or register your teen for the exam.

Questions parents ask

Can you do high school chemistry at home without a lab?

Many of the core ideas can be taught with safe, adult-run household demonstrations, and our intro plan is built that way. A full lab course with chemicals, glassware and safety gear is a different thing. If your teen needs lab credit, plan for a co-op, a community college or a lab kit with its own safety guidance.

Is this a full homeschool chemistry curriculum for high school credit?

Intro to Chemistry is a 12-week course for ages 11 to 15, so it is a foundation, not a full high school credit on its own. Ace AP Chemistry is a 12-week study plan for the exam, meant to be used with your full course. How either counts on a transcript is your family's call.

What should my teen know before high school chemistry?

Atoms, molecules, states of matter, the signs of a chemical change and a first look at the periodic table. Comfort with basic algebra helps too. Our intro chemistry placement check shows which of these are solid.

Can a teen study AP Chemistry on their own?

A motivated teen can run much of the study plan alone. AP Chemistry is demanding, so most students also need a full course or textbook, and the plan is built to be used alongside one.
