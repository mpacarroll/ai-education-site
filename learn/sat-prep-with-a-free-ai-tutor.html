SAT and PSAT Prep With a Free AI Tutor: A 12-Week Plan

How to use ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini for SAT and PSAT prep: a diagnostic first, then targeted weekly practice instead of random question sets.

2026-11-02

Most SAT prep wastes time on questions a student already gets right. The fix is the same with or without AI: find the weak skills first, then practice those.

Week 1: diagnose, don't drill

A good start is a short diagnostic across the skills the test checks, with each question labeled by skill. The point is not a score. It is a list: these skills are solid, these are gaps, start here. Our SAT course opens with exactly that, run by the AI tutor in one session.

How to use AI for SAT prep

AI assistants are strong at explanation and patient practice. Ask the tutor to work like this:

  • One question at a time, labeled with the skill it tests.
  • Ask before explaining. "What did you notice first?" before "here's how to solve it."
  • Explain mistakes by asking. "Which choice did you rule out first, and why?"

What AI is bad at, and what to do about it

AI assistants sometimes write a multiple-choice question with no correct answer, or two. So:

  • Tell the tutor to work out the answer first, then build the choices, then check that exactly one works. Our prompts do this.
  • Use AI for learning, and use the College Board's official practice tests for timed scoring.
  • When something looks wrong, ask the AI to check it. Catching an error is good test practice in itself.

A sensible 12-week shape

  1. Weeks 1 to 2: diagnose, then set the plan.
  2. Weeks 3 to 9: one or two weak skills a week, practice, and a short checkpoint.
  3. Weeks 10 to 12: full timed sections, review of mistakes, and test-day habits.

Questions parents ask

Can ChatGPT replace an SAT tutor?

For many students it can cover most of what a tutor does: diagnose weak skills, explain, and give targeted practice. It cannot replace official practice tests, which you should still take under timed conditions.

Are AI-written SAT questions reliable?

Not always. AI can write a question with no correct choice, or two. Use AI for explanation and skill practice, and use the official practice tests from the College Board for scoring yourself.

How long before the SAT should a student start preparing?

About three months of steady weekly work is a common plan, which is why our course runs twelve weeks.
