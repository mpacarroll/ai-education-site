Homeschool Placement Test: Free Checks by Subject

Free homeschool placement tests by subject, run online with any AI chat assistant: math, algebra, reading, writing, science and history. No sign-up.

2026-11-26

A homeschool placement test answers one question: where should my child start? Not which grade they are in, but which skills are solid and which are not. Get that right and almost any curriculum works better. Get it wrong and a good curriculum feels too hard or too easy by week two.

This page is the hub for every free placement check on the site, grouped by subject. If you want the reasoning first, why placement matters more than which curriculum you pick explains it, and the free math placement test page walks through one check from start to report.

How the checks work

Each check is one prompt you paste into an AI chat assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot, free versions included. The AI asks your child questions one at a time, gives no hints or feedback during the check, and writes a short report at the end with the week to start at. About 15 minutes, no sign-up, nothing to install.

Who this is for

  • Parents starting homeschool, or switching curriculum mid-year.
  • Parents of a child who seems behind, or ahead, and want to know where.
  • Teens who want to place themselves before an AP or SAT plan.

Reading the report

Every report lists each question, what your child answered, and a score per area built only from those answers. It names the week to start at and the two gaps that matter most for the first month. If nothing was wrong, it says so rather than inventing a gap. Save the report: if you use the course, you paste it under the launch prompt for that week.

What the checks do not do

  • They do not give a grade level, percentile or score to compare with other children.
  • They are not a standardized test, and they do not meet any state testing requirement on their own. Check your own state's rules.
  • They do not teach. Teaching starts in the course, on the week the report names.

Questions parents ask

Is there a free homeschool placement test?

Yes. Every guide on this site has a free placement check you run online with any AI chat assistant, including the free versions. There is no sign-up, no email and nothing to download. It takes about 15 minutes.

Is there a homeschool placement test for high school?

Yes. There are checks for algebra, geometry, history, writing and each AP exam we cover, plus the SAT, PSAT and ACT. Older students can usually run their own.

Can I print the placement test as a PDF?

The check is a conversation, so it runs in the AI chat rather than on paper. The report it writes at the end can be saved or printed, and the paid course download includes the check as a file too.

How is a placement check different from a standardized test?

A standardized test compares your child with others of the same age. A placement check only answers where to start in one course, and names the week. It gives no grade and no percentile.
