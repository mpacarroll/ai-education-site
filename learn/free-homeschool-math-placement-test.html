A Free Homeschool Math Placement Test You Run With Any AI

How to find your child's real math level, from arithmetic through pre-algebra, in about 15 minutes with a free AI chat assistant and no sign-up.

2026-11-02

Math is the subject where placement matters most. Every new skill rests on an earlier one, so a child who never quite got place value will struggle with long division, and a child shaky on fractions will struggle with algebra. Grade level does not tell you where those gaps are. A placement check does.

What the check does

You paste one prompt into any AI chat assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot, free versions included. The assistant then:

  • asks your child a few setup questions;
  • works through core math from arithmetic toward pre-algebra, one question at a time;
  • says only "Thanks, got it" between questions, with no hints, no teaching and no "correct";
  • stops early in an area once it's clear, so a strong learner moves on quickly.

After about 15 minutes it writes a report you can save.

Why "no hints" matters

If the AI helps during a placement check, you learn what your child can do with help, not alone, and the start week comes out too high. The check's rules tell the AI to hold back even if your child or you ask for a hint. "That comes after the check" is its whole answer.

Reading the report

The report lists every question, your child's actual answer, and right, wrong or not answered. Each area gets a score built only from that list. Then it names:

  • the week to start at in the 12-week course;
  • the two gaps that matter most for the first month.

A correct answer never shows up as a gap, and if nothing was wrong, the report says "no gaps seen yet" instead of inventing one.

What to do next

Save the report. If you use the course, paste the report under the launch prompt for the week it names, and the AI tutor picks up from what the check found. If you use other curriculum, the report still tells you which unit to go back to.

Questions parents ask

What does the math placement check cover?

Core math understanding from arithmetic through pre-algebra. It asks a few questions in each area, one at a time, and finds the earliest gap, because in math a missing earlier skill causes most later trouble.

Does my child need to know they're being tested?

It helps to call it a check, not a test. There's no grade, the AI gives no right-or-wrong feedback during it, and the point is to find where to start, not to pass.

What do I get at the end?

A short report: each question, what your child answered, a score for each area, the week of the course to start at, and the two gaps that matter most for the first month.
