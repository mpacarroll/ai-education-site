How to Check Your Child's Reading Level at Home, Free

Two free AI placement checks, one for early readers and one for ages 8 to 12, that show where your child is and which week of reading to start at.

2026-11-02

Reading level is two different questions depending on age. For a five-year-old, it's "can they turn letters into words?" For a ten-year-old who reads fluently, it's "do they understand what they read, and can they say why?" We have a free check for each.

For early readers (about ages 5 to 8)

The reading readiness check looks at the skills that come before fluent reading: letters and their sounds, blending sounds into words, common sight words, and reading a short sentence. You sit with your child the whole time. The AI asks a question; you read it aloud if needed, and type what your child says.

It leads into Learning to Read, which runs from Meeting the Alphabet in Week 1 through blending, sight words, first sentences and little stories, to a Week 12 story time where your child reads a book they chose.

For readers who need to understand more (about ages 8 to 12)

Plenty of children read every word on the page and miss what the page means. The reading comprehension check gives a short passage and asks questions about it: what happened, why, what the author meant, what the child would predict. It shows which comprehension skills are solid and which are missing.

It leads into Read to Understand, where the AI tutor asks questions about real reading instead of explaining it.

How both checks work

  • Paste one prompt into any AI chat assistant. Free versions work.
  • The AI asks one question at a time and gives no hints or feedback during the check.
  • After about 15 minutes, it writes a report: each question, the answer given, a score for each area, and the week to start.

Save the report. It goes under that week's launch prompt if you use the course.

Tips for an honest result

  • Don't help. If your child is stuck, let the check record it. That's the information you need.
  • Pick a good time. A tired child reads below their level.
  • Keep it light. Call it a reading game, not a test.

Questions parents ask

Which reading check should I use?

For a child who is learning to read, roughly ages 5 to 8, use the reading readiness check. For a child who reads but may not understand deeply, roughly ages 8 to 12, use the reading comprehension check.

Can an AI really judge my young child's reading?

It needs your help. For early readers, you sit with your child, read the AI's questions aloud if needed, and type what your child says or reads. The AI scores what you report, so be honest about hesitations.

How often should I recheck reading level?

At natural breaks: the start of a term, after winter break, or when reading suddenly feels harder or easier.
